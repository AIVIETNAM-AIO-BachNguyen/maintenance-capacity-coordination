# Research brief

## Current objective

Develop and evaluate a framework that coordinates each machine's maintenance need/value representation with heterogeneous technician capacity and current service commitments. The operational decision is whether to keep a machine productive and exposed to future failure or start service that causes downtime and occupies a particular technician for multiple ticks. A standard PPO/centralized-training algorithm is sufficient; a new optimizer is not required.

The research question is whether a useful machine-demand representation combined with skill, service-duration and busy-commitment information improves economic decisions, sample efficiency or robustness against strong same-information, same-action-set controls. A machine embedding alone is a structured centralized policy, not a separate machine actor or a calibrated economic value. Do not add a redundant binary requester action merely to call the design MARL.

## Evidence so far

Pilot v1 implemented shared binary PM requesters and a fully informed masked coordinator on unchanged physics. Its sealed TEST confirmed lower H+tail cost for capacity-aware C than learned always-request D, but C did not establish a stable capacity-feedback advantage over local B, centralized E, frozen V14, risk matching or rollout. The separate exploratory G diagnostic found fixed threshold requests plus learned coordinator better than C on its own panel. Under the pilot budget B/C under-requested and D over-served; these observations do not prove machine information or PPO intrinsically useless. V1's labelled “replication” was held-out physical evaluation of existing checkpoints, not training replication. V1 TEST is closed to all new design choices.

The first revised design, `docs/demand_capacity_commitment_proposal_v2.md`, preserved threshold requests but allowed just-in-time PM submission for an extra healthy machine selected for immediate service. Its sole prelocked headroom check on F2-visited states and common independent forecasts passed audit but failed the minimum-effect gate: expanded decisions improved six-tick holdout forecast cost by only 0.44 units versus a required 2.0. See `docs/commitment_headroom_v2_findings.md`. No v2 learner or scientific learning pilot was opened.

## Research constraints and next step

Keep the simulator's failure law, restoration, technician skills, non-preemptive service, automatic CM and economic objective intact. Do not create fees, forced shutdowns, health concealment or future-event inputs. A coordinator must retain feasible matching/WAIT choices; any new action-set or operational contract must be versioned and separated from architecture effects. Full-information centralized PPO, threshold + learned coordinator G, always-request + learned coordinator D, direct-health representation, frozen V14, risk matching and rollout are required controls or clearly labelled external references for a qualified pilot. Compare matched information, action set, parameter/sample budgets, forecast compute and wall time.

A distinct same-action structural-learning pilot in `docs/structured_demand_commitment_pilot_v3.md` has now been implemented and evaluated. Shared machine-demand and worker-commitment encoders scored complete threshold-admitted matchings; a flat full-information PPO received the same raw inputs and action mask, while a matched ablation removed only numerical remaining-time context. The fixed-threshold request pool did not reopen v2 early-PM eligibility. DEV and held-out audits passed, but S−G3 missed its prelocked practical gain threshold and S−R showed no remaining-time benefit. See `docs/structured_demand_commitment_pilot_v3_findings.md`. Independent training replication and new TEST were not opened. A further experiment needs a distinct, justified operational mechanism and new locked protocol, not adjustment of v3 after outcomes. Physical episodes sharing a tape are paired clusters; training seeds are separate sources of variation. A new TEST requires separate user authorization. Do not expand N/K until the decoder, environment and strong controls support it. A negative result is retained rather than repaired by unplanned modules or revised gates.

The v5 bounded interaction check in `docs/pair_commitment_interaction_check_v5_findings.md` passed its independent-bank gate for reciprocal simultaneous service starts: an additive decomposition lost +2.91 six-tick forecast cost within 151 two-start-eligible states, with episode-cluster interval [+1.53,+4.65]. This was representation headroom, not realized H+tail evidence. The separately prelocked v6 pilot gave the explicit two-edge student J6, additive ablation A6 and flat full-information student G6 identical F2 root-score training data and budgets, with F2/V14/risk/G/D same-panel references. The v6 gate failed; no independent training replication or new TEST is eligible. The desired validated capacity-coordination benefit remains unproven. The later same-tick sequential matching series is summarized in `CURRENT_STATE.md`. V8's unchanged-physics C–D PPO pilot and v10's predeclared high-demand simulation pilot both passed replay audits but missed their locked practical feedback gates; v9's simulation-only opportunity qualification failed its joint case gate and did not train PPO. In v10 C10−D10 was −12.58 H+tail but C10−G10 was +22.60, and same-weight within-tick freezing changed only two greedy matchings on 9,216 C10-visited H states. These findings preserve the goal as **unproven** and identify sparse conditional choices at N4/K2 as a concrete limitation. Simulated trajectories remain the intended RL training data; real operational logs are optional calibration. That finding motivated a distinct, versioned N5/K3 simulation regime with matching-capable strong controls before its outcomes, without revising the v8–v10 gates or opening TEST.

The framework remains unproven. V17 then separately prelocked complete-matching simulator continuation labels across H, including WAIT, singleton STOP and three-pair choices, while holding the v16 graph actor and physical contract fixed. Source tests, bounded Ubuntu smokes and independent label, train/DEV and fresh held-out replay audits passed. The bank contained 508 training states/16,128 forced branches; completed training used 60 trials/491,520 H decisions. On held-out C17−D17 was +8.17 H+tail [+0.28,+15.95] versus the locked ≤−20 gate, and same-weight C17−CF17 was −1.01 [−5.27,+3.40] versus ≤−10 and upper <0. C17 lost to I/R/B/S/G overall. The bank repaired target coverage, yet an in-sample diagnostic found C17 candidate-list cross-entropy approximately uniform after pretraining. That observation does not identify why the physical result failed. A fixed 32-row training-only overfit check reduced C17 cross-entropy to 1.844 versus target entropy 1.735 after 512 updates, indicating some ranking capacity without demonstrating full-bank generalization or physical gain. A future experiment needs a distinct training-only uncertainty, fresh prelock and matched controls; simulation remains sufficient for RL training, and real logs are optional later calibration.
