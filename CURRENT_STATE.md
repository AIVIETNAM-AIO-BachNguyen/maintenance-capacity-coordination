## Next research decision

The desired validated maintenance-demand/technician-capacity coordination benefit remains unproven under the implemented full-information start-service contract. Stop new synthetic training campaigns on the v1–v7 outcomes. The one next step is to obtain evidence of a real operational decision absent from this simulator—such as binding technician or machine commitments *before* service starts, with actual timing/cancellation and production consequences. If confirmed, specify a versioned contract and prelock a small diagnostic with the same-information/action centralized and always-request controls before any learner or TEST. If no such requirement exists, report the audited negative/mixed result rather than changing the simulator to force a gain.
