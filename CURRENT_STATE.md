## Active research boundary

The physical simulator, failure law, restoration, heterogeneous skills, non-preemptive service, PM/CM lifecycle, threshold PM admission, H48+tail16 cost and legal complete matchings were unchanged in v16. The canonical decoder builds a provisional matching within a tick; it does not advance time or incur cost between tokens. The contribution under test was whether recomputing incidence-aware demand/capacity messages after a provisional pair improves the completed matching. Sequential decisions, STOP and graph message passing are prior art; this is an empirical framework claim, not a new RL algorithm or an implemented two-policy requester/coordinator.
