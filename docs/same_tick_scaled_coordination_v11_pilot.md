# Scaled same-tick contextual coordination v11 — prelocked simulation pilot

Status: LOCKED BEFORE V11 SCIENTIFIC TRAINING OR EVALUATION OUTCOMES, 2026-10-23 (Asia/Taipei). Engineering design: `same_tick_scaled_coordination_v11_design.md`. Earlier v1–v10 gates and verdicts remain closed. No new TEST is authorized.

## Operating contract and hypothesis

Use **both** fixed, pre-existing simulator-family configurations `n5k3_sparse_a` and `n5k3_sparse_b` from `family_config("n5_k3_sparse", seed, "full")` with configuration seeds `73740000` and `73740001`, respectively; replace horizon by 64 solely to retain H48+tail16. The generator fixes N5/K3, age threshold 3, failure probability .60, 2–4-tick positive service durations with sparse physical compatibility, and its existing restoration distribution. The two configurations are fixed before outcomes; do not select a case after smoke, DEV or held-out performance. The existing `RequestEnv` implements threshold PM, automatic CM, non-preemptive service, failure/restoration/cost law and simultaneous service starts. Only the final complete matching executes physically once per tick. No artificial requests, fees, hidden information, forced shutdowns, reservation or event-tape inputs. The setting is a simulated resource-allocation regime, not factory calibration.

Hypothesis: when three heterogeneous technicians permit several residual assignments, updating remaining machine-demand, technician-capacity and provisional selected-edge context after each within-tick assignment yields lower H+tail economic cost than keeping that context fixed. The primary estimand is equal-case, equal-training-root, paired-physical-seed **C11−D11** H+tail cost, negative favorable. A paired **same-weight** counterfactual compares each selected C11 checkpoint with its own weights but frozen within-tick context (CF11) on the identical fresh physical tapes; C11−CF11 tests whether the deployed contextual update itself changes economic outcomes. It is an inference ablation, not an independently trained policy.

## Actors, action space and probability

All same-admission scratch arms receive full present-state N5/K3 information, the same `catalogue(5,3)` of 136 physical partial matchings including WAIT, the same H/tail reward and training H-decision budget:

| Arm | Role |
| --- | --- |
| C11 | Shared machine/worker encoders; sequential edge/STOP scorer, recomputing partial-matching context after every edge |
| D11 | Identical architecture/parameters/input; initial context frozen within the tick, feasibility still refreshed |
| B11 | Direct-feature sequential representation control on the same internal edge/STOP action set |
| S11 | Shared-entity structured complete-matching PPO control |
| G11 | Flat full-information complete-matching PPO control |
| CF11 | Selected C11 weights loaded into D11 only at inference; no training or checkpoint selection |
| Q11 | Flat full-information PPO with always-request PM admission; different request/matching availability, contextual not architecture attribution |
| F1/F2 | Threshold + risk matching and threshold + six-tick rollout on the same simulator/physical tapes |

The historical frozen V14 checkpoint is dimension-locked to N4/K2 (56 inputs, 21 actions). It cannot legally score the N5/K3 panel, so it remains an external N4/K2 reference rather than a projected or mislabelled same-panel control. G11 and F2 provide full-action same-panel learned and forecast-heavy controls. Report this limitation when interpreting practicality.

For C11/D11/B11, PPO's random variable is the **ordered sequence** of masked feasible edges and STOP, with one physical transition/reward assigned to the completed matching. Exact conditional sequence log probabilities sum for the old/new PPO ratio. Different sequences may map to the same matching; do not label sequence log probability a matching marginal or sampled-path entropy matching entropy. STOP at root means WAIT, after any edge means a partial matching, and exhausted edges force termination without another sampled token. Deterministic inference greedily chooses the next token and may not maximize physical-matching probability. S11/G11/Q11 use one masked categorical over complete matchings. No decoder step advances time, consumes an event, charges cost or makes a physical commitment.

## Metrics, locked gate and progression

Primary: paired selected-policy H+tail cost C11−D11 and paired same-checkpoint C11−CF11, equal across cases, three roots and 32 physical seeds. Bootstrap the **raw physical seed ID as one cluster** across cases, arms and roots (5,000 percentile resamples), conditional on trained roots; report per-case and per-root means separately. Secondary: C−B/S/G, C−Q contextual comparison, C−F1/F2, H availability/failures, PM/CM queues/backlog, service starts, WAIT, matching cardinality, generalist/resource allocation, learning curves, inference latency, actor/critic parameter count, scorer calls, training wall time and F2 forecast transitions. Report root-state and chosen-first-edge residual-choice frequencies, second-token rank/action changes under same-weight freezing, and matching-level outcomes. Changed action rate alone is not economic gain; do not sum local regrets into episode gain.

Independent training replication is eligible only if **all** of the following pass on the audited held-out panel: C11−D11 overall ≤−20 H+tail, both case means <0, at least two of three root means <0, physical-cluster 95% interval upper endpoint <0; C11−B11 and C11−G11 overall <0; and same-weight C11−CF11 overall ≤−10, both case means <0 and cluster interval upper endpoint <0. These thresholds are locked before scientific outcomes, preserving the v8/v10 practical C−D threshold and requiring an actual deployed feedback effect. If any condition fails, close v11 without independent retraining or TEST. If all pass, retrain all six scratch arms on reserved roots with unchanged source/protocol, then evaluate on fresh paired seeds; require negative C−D, C−G and C−CF11 overall, both C−D and C−CF11 case means negative, and both cluster upper endpoints <0 before separately requesting TEST authorization. Strong F2 comparison limits practical claims even if attribution passes; a C11 cost more than 20 above F2 overall is not a strong deployment result under this protocol. No TEST entry point may run without separate explicit authorization.
