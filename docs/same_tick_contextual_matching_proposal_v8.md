# Same-tick demand–capacity feedback: v8 proposal

Status: formulation fixed before v8 implementation or learning outcomes, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). This is a new, unchanged-physics study directed by the current goal objective. V1–v7 verdicts and their TEST panels remain closed.

## Question and inherited contract

At each physical tick, threshold PM submissions and automatic CM create the admitted queue. A coordinator constructs one legal partial matching, including WAIT, and the existing simulator executes it once. Selected pairs inside the decoder are *provisional*: they do not occupy a resource, advance the failure tape, produce cost, or restore a machine until the final matching is passed to the existing `CoordinationEnv.step`. Service remains non-preemptive. H=48 plus the inherited 16-tick tail, failure law, costs and physical action set stay unchanged. No binary requester is learned.

V3 S uses shared machine and worker encoders, then scores each complete matching against a pooled representation of the initial state. It can express joint effects through its action scorer, but its state representation does not respond to a first selected pair before scoring the second. V6 added explicit pair scoring and failed against the flat control. V7 showed case-dependent S–G3 differences and near-identical S–R behavior. Those outcomes neither prove nor refute whether *within-tick contextual feedback* helps a sequential policy.

The proposed C8 decoder first encodes every machine's current demand and each technician's current capacity with shared MLPs. At each internal step it scores every remaining feasible machine–technician edge and STOP. After choosing an edge, it removes that machine and technician and recomputes a context vector from (i) the remaining machine embeddings, (ii) the remaining worker embeddings and (iii) the embeddings and known service-duration/restoration features of provisional selected edges. That context enters the next edge and STOP logits, so the first pair can change relative scores of the remaining pairs beyond a legality mask. D8 has **identical trainable modules, raw observations, initial logits and mask sequence**, but keeps the initial context fixed throughout the tick. It still removes selected machines and technicians from feasibility. C8–D8 is the primary feedback attribution contrast. A direct-feature sequential B8 receives the same raw input and sequence action set but has no shared learned demand embedding. It diagnoses whether the representation adds anything beyond sequential decisions. V3 S and flat G3 are retrained under the same new training seed, physical action set and H-decision budget; frozen V14, risk matching and F2 rollout are strong contextual references.

C8's output is a centralized matching policy, not a two-policy requester/coordinator framework and not calibrated machine value. It inherits recursive assignment and STOP/skip ideas already present in Wang and Gombolay's [AirME/HetGPO](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/19839/19839-40-23852-1-2-20220613.pdf) and [source implementation](https://github.com/CORE-Robotics-Lab/AirME). The specific question is whether *recomputed remaining-demand/remaining-capacity/selected-commitment context* improves this simulator's economic matching decisions under matched controls. Sequential decoding, graph/entity embeddings, PPO and resource reservation by themselves are not novelty claims. The prior AirME setting has its own aircraft, crew and failure model, so its reported performance does not transfer to this simulator.

## Probability and falsification

The sampled **ordered internal sequence** is the policy action for PPO. Its probability is the product of masked conditional probabilities through STOP or forced termination. The physical environment sees the unordered final matching once, and the one physical reward/return is assigned to the whole sampled sequence. Different orders may yield the same matching; the sequence log probability is never labelled the marginal matching probability. PPO ratios use exactly the sampled sequence under old and updated policies. Entropy is the sum of conditional entropies along the sampled path and is labelled sequence entropy. Deterministic evaluation uses greedy next-token decoding, which need not produce the highest-probability *matching*. This choice preserves the existing matching/WAIT action set and keeps the pilot small while making order multiplicity explicit.

The primary falsifier is C8–D8 paired H+tail cost: a C8 gain against flat G3 without a C8–D8 gain does not validate feedback. If C8 beats D8 only through a changed action histogram but not matching-level or episode outcomes, feedback usefulness is unproven. If B8 matches C8, a demand-embedding contribution is unproven. If gains fail on either prespecified case, or vanish across independent training seeds, the claim is limited or rejected. Mechanism analysis will record changed second-step scores/actions and paired episode outcomes without treating action-change rate, attention weights, or sums of local regrets as economic gain.
