# Score–decoder alignment diagnostic: proposed prelock

Status: **design locked in this document; no scientific collection has run**. This is the single current diagnostic. It does not reopen V1–V22, train an actor, alter the simulator or authorize TEST. Source/checkpoint identities below must be hash-verified before a run; any mismatch is an integrity stop, not a reason to substitute another checkpoint.

## Question, falsifiable hypothesis and interpretation

The fixed C21 actor assigns a complete canonical matching the sum of its conditional token log probabilities, whereas deployed deterministic decoding picks the locally largest legal token at each step. The hypothesis is that these two rules select different matchings often enough, and the full-sequence-score choice has at least a 10-unit lower H48+tail16 continuation cost on independent future tapes in each fixed case. If the choices seldom differ or their independently measured cost gap misses the gate below, the proposed execution-gap mechanism is not qualified. Even a pass shows only a one-decision, S16-continuation diagnostic opportunity, not an achievable whole-episode policy gain or a demand–capacity feedback contribution.

Keep the unchanged `n5k3_residual_a/b` configuration, seeds 74740000/74740001, threshold admission, full present-state information, non-preemptive PM/CM, matching/WAIT action set, H48+tail16 accounting and C21 selected checkpoints at roots 82810000..82810002. No future-event realization enters the policy input, candidate construction or score. The fixed continuation evaluator is the audited S16 checkpoint mapped by root index as in V20. Do not tune C21 weights, select checkpoints, alter a skill mask or change decoder for physical evaluation here.

## Exact same-state comparisons

For each admitted state, enumerate every legal complete physical matching, including WAIT. Map each to its unique increasing-edge canonical sequence, with STOP where another canonical edge remains feasible; when no edge remains, termination is implicit. Verify one-to-one matching coverage, token legality, masks, STOP position and zero/one/multiple-pair cases. For each matching, compute the actor's exact conditional canonical path log probability with `SequencePolicy.logprob_entropy`, preserving its within-tick graph refresh. Independently reconstruct the sum of masked log-softmax token terms. Compare these scores with the deployed `sample(..., deterministic=True)` greedy matching and its own path log probability. If any reconstruction, probability, score, checkpoint or action-validity check fails, stop before economic analysis and preserve the failure artifact. A canonical sequence score is the matching probability only under this canonical policy; do not call it an unrestricted-order marginal.

Let G be the deployed greedy matching, S the **global** maximum complete-sequence score over all legal matchings (tie: lowest catalogue index), and O_A the minimum mean-cost matching in the labeled candidate set on Bank A (tie: lowest catalogue index). Include G and S in the candidate set before any future tape is sampled, plus WAIT, F1's matching if distinct, one matching of each available cardinality 1/2/3 in catalogue order, then catalogue-order fill to at most eight unique matchings. If mandatory G/S/WAIT/F1 members exceed eight, retain all mandatory members and stop with a protocol error; do not drop one. Report whether each option is in the fixed candidate bank, the total legal matching count and the bank fraction. Because S is the global score maximum and is included, the score-vs-greedy identity comparison covers the full legal action set. O_A is an oracle **within the candidate bank only**; no claim about the global economic optimum follows.

Primary per-row estimands, in H+tail units with positive favoring S, are `mean_B[cost(G)-cost(S)]` and `mean_B[cost(S)-cost(O_A)]`. Scores and G/S identities use only the present state. O_A is chosen using Bank A and valued using independent Bank B future-event tapes. If G=S, their cost difference is exactly zero and only one branch need be simulated. Secondary measures: G≠S frequency; complete-sequence log-score gap `log p(S)-log p(G)`; bank-conditional top-score versus Bank-A/B cost-winner agreement; pairwise score/cost sign agreement for cost gaps ≥20; per-case and per-root results; STOP and cardinality strata; Bank-A/B sign stability. Report Bank-B candidate spread and G/S/O_A regret without multiplying a local continuation gap into episode return. Include distinct-state and distinct-episode counts.

Split its 16 tapes into first eight and last eight only for descriptive label stability. Both halves were part of C21 training, so Stage A cannot validate generalization or qualify an intervention. Stage B uses **new diagnostic-only** raw physical seeds 84801000..84801023, 24 F1 episodes per case. For each episode take the first H tick with at least one feasible edge and at least two legal complete matchings; if none, record an unqualified episode. Freeze that admitted present state. For each unique candidate force it once and continue with its root-mapped S16 policy on 16 common future-event tapes, numbered 0..15, generated by role-separated deterministic seeds from the raw seed, case, selected tick and tape index. ## Locked decision branches and campaign stop

1. **Decoder mechanism qualifies for a separately prelocked pilot proposal** only if audit PASS, coverage above, G≠S in at least 10% of qualified rows in **each** case, `cost_B(G)-cost_B(S) ≥10` in **each** case, equal-case pooled cluster interval lower endpoint >0, and `cost_B(S)-cost_B(O_A) ≤5` in each case. This does not authorize implementing a new decoder or running physical pilot outcomes under this protocol.
2. **Ranking/fit uncertainty goes to a separate Stage-2 prelock** if decoder qualification fails and `cost_B(S)-cost_B(O_A) ≥10` in both cases with pooled interval lower endpoint >0, provided O_A's Bank-B advantage over S has the expected sign in at least two of three roots. The Stage-2 design must distinguish tiny-set fit, full-bank fit, separate validation and PPO drift before any learner change.
3. **Mixed or insufficient evidence stops this campaign.** If both gaps are small, coverage is inadequate, labels are unstable or case/root results fail the fixed conditions, record a scoped negative/inconclusive verdict. Do not open a learner, alter thresholds, search another mask, increase tape budget or inspect old TEST. A new question would need independent grounds and a new prelock.

Total new diagnostic simulation budget is capped above; there is no PPO, independent retraining, held-out physical policy panel or TEST budget in this protocol. Any later decoder intervention must state its changed rule, keep weights/physics/action set fixed initially, compare dynamic/frozen and full-information controls where applicable, and prelock paired episode-level H+tail gates, compute and root stability before collection.
