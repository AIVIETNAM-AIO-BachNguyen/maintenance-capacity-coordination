# Post-v6 formulation decision

Date: 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). This is an evidence synthesis and decision about the next experiment, not a new protocol or an amendment to any prior gate. Scientific source and artifacts are identified in `joint_demand_capacity_pilot_v6_findings.md`. V1 TEST remains closed; v2–v4 and v6 failed gates remain in force.

## What the implemented contract permits

`CoordinationEnv.step` in `src/maintenance_capacity_coordination/coordination_v1.py` first validates PM submissions and a complete legal matching/WAIT action, then calls the persistent-request simulator. `submit_requests` in `src/ht_pdm_fjsp/maintenance_requests.py` creates a queued PM request without occupying a technician. `legal_matchings` enumerates partial matchings only among queued requests and physically compatible free technician–machine edges. The physical transition and economic cost depend on the service-start matching, current physical state and event tape. Automatic CM is created or upgraded after failure. A queued PM machine continues operating; only starting service takes the machine and technician out of use.

Thus a free extra request expands the *immediate* matching catalogue and can be ignored by choosing another matching or WAIT. This is a statement about physical choices at a tick, not equality of trained policies or whole H+tail episodes. Request creation time enters `risk_matching` through a wait-age term; persistent extra requests also change the fixed risk-matching tail's queue. Hence different request histories can lead to different forecast/tail decisions even when earlier physical states and chosen starts coincide. No exact H+tail equivalence between threshold and always-request follows from immediate action inclusion. Conversely, these request-history effects do not establish that a learned requester improves economic decisions against a fully informed coordinator.

Current full-information matching policies already observe present machine health, skill/service durations, technician remaining commitments and the legal action mask. Their physical decision can start simultaneous services or WAIT. Calling an embedding a calibrated maintenance value or an independent machine policy would require different implementation and validation. Any proposed advantage under this contract must therefore be empirical learning efficiency, robustness or cost against matched full-information controls; it cannot be inferred from access to a unique service-start action.

## Evidence and the remaining gap

- V1 showed a narrow capacity-aware requester C gain over learned always-request D, but no stable gain over local B, centralized E or strong rules; the separate threshold + learned coordinator G diagnostic favored G over C. The v1 TEST is closed to design selection.
- V2's just-in-time early-PM option missed its predeclared six-tick forecast-headroom threshold. Its negative result does not prove zero headroom in every state or contract.
- V3's structured PPO had a conditional paired gain over flat PPO but missed its locked practical threshold and showed almost no numerical remaining-time ablation benefit. V4 forecast distillation and v6 explicit pair scoring did not establish superiority over matched flat controls. V5's independent-bank pair interaction was forecast headroom relative to an additive approximation; v6 did not turn it into a realized learning benefit.
- In the final audited v6 panel, J6−G6 was +30.80 H+tail with a conditional physical-cluster interval [+15.28,+46.67], and J6's reciprocal F2 pair-error was 1.88 times A6's versus the locked ≤0.80 requirement. J6's large gain over A6 occurred alongside A6's reciprocal service-WAIT collapse. These observations do not justify another score architecture or a lowered v6 gate.

There is no currently evidenced same-contract mechanism with a predeclared practical advantage over the strong full-information or rollout controls. A new scorer, loss, label source, horizon, seed panel or larger N/K launched from these outcomes would be outcome-guided search. Repeating v6 with more favorable seeds would not be independent confirmation.

## Decision and condition for a new campaign

Stop scientific runs at v6. Preserve every raw artifact and negative finding; run no v6 independent training replication or new TEST. The evaluated framework is correctly described as a two-stage v1 policy and later structured centralized matching policies, with limited or negative benefit claims. The desired validated capacity-coordination advantage remains unproven.

A genuinely new experiment needs evidence of an operational decision absent from the current start-service contract, or an independently motivated deployment requirement that the current full-information controls cannot already meet. One possible question to validate with the domain owner is whether technicians or machines make binding *pre-start* scheduling commitments, and what cancellation, timing and production consequences actually exist. This is not an approved simulator change. If that operational fact is confirmed, write a versioned contract that states its decision order and physical/economic consequences, retain same-information/action centralized and always-request controls, and prelock a bounded headroom check before a learner pilot. If no such distinct mechanism is supported, report the present negative/mixed evaluation as the research outcome rather than run another architecture campaign.
