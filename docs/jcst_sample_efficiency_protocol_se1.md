## Question, hypotheses and estimands

Does the component–technician structured actor reach lower physical cost with fewer simulator decisions than a same-information flat actor, and does that save elapsed training time? Does cross-entity messaging add benefit beyond the structured actor without it? S−F measures the **whole structured actor package**, A−F the structured package without cross-message inputs, and S−A the incremental cross-message contribution. A negative result is retained; an interval containing zero does not establish equivalence. Cases are reported separately before an equal-case descriptive summary.

The primary outcome for each arm, case and independent training root is the cost area over 24k–384k controlled decisions. At each of the eight locked checkpoints, first average undiscounted H+tail episode cost over the same 128 paired physical seeds. Integrate those eight means by trapezoids against natural log of sample budget and divide by `log(384000)-log(24000)`. The result has units of mean simulator cost; lower is better. Equal-weight the ten root areas within each arm/case, then form S−F, A−F and S−A in each case. Checkpoints and physical seeds are repeated measures, not new training roots. This window does not imply behavior at budgets outside it.

The six primary contrasts form one family. Use 10,000 crossed-bootstrap draws with RNG seed 20261028: independently resample ten training roots within each arm, jointly resample 128 physical-seed indices across all arms, and retain each selected root's entire checkpoint trajectory. Recompute root areas in every draw. Report two-sided percentile intervals at `1-0.05/6` coverage (99.1667%) and raw means. A practical advantage for a contrast in a case requires observed mean difference ≤−10 cost units and adjusted upper bound <0. An across-core-cases claim requires both case-specific contrasts to pass. Do not replace a failed area gate with a favorable endpoint. The two-case descriptive equal-case result has no separate success gate. Six contrasts remain reported even if earlier L2/L3 conclusions are negative.

## Fixed physical and learning contract

## Training and evaluation panel

Train from scratch for 2 cases × 3 arms × 10 new independent roots. Each root visits nested checkpoints `24k, 48k, 72k, 96k, 144k, 192k, 288k, 384k` controlled decisions, corresponding to 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000, 3,000, 4,000, 6,000 and 8,000 complete H=48 episodes. Save every checkpoint and training elapsed time. Each root gets its own physical TRAIN and Torch streams; an episode may not be resumed or counted twice. Run all roots regardless of intermediate cost. Evaluate all eight greedy checkpoints on the **same** 128 fresh physical evaluation seeds per case, shared across arms, roots and checkpoints. Evaluate threshold5, risk/duration matching, corrective-only and WAIT once per physical seed and case on that same bank. Preserve per-episode H cost, tail cost, cost components, PM/CM, failures, downtime, backlog, legal/action diagnostics, checkpoint identity and full replay trace. Report every root/checkpoint mean and trajectory, including collapses, regressions and crossings.

Secondary stochastic deployment is fixed at 96k and 384k for every arm/root/case: four independent action seeds per physical seed with the same physical tape. Average the four repetitions within physical seed for summaries; report within-seed variation separately. Stochastic outcomes cannot replace the greedy primary. The learned-policy evaluation panel is never used for tuning or choosing a checkpoint.
