# V18 matching-value precision: locked successor

Status: **locked before v18 implementation, smoke, labels, train/DEV or held-out outcomes**. V1–v17 gates and negative verdicts remain closed. V18 is the one current experiment. Simulator data alone supply labels and PPO trajectories; real logs are not a prerequisite. No new TEST is authorized.

## Distinct uncertainty and contribution boundary

V17's full-matching label bank repaired coverage but C17's supervised fit was near a uniform candidate predictor, and audited physical C17−D17/CF17 failed. A subsequent **training-bank-only** split of its four independent future tapes found the best candidate agreed across halves in 19.0%/23.0% of the two cases, while candidate-pair difference signs agreed in 55.7%/59.3%. The four-tape winner gap was often smaller than tape variation. A 32-row overfit showed C17 can learn some rankings with more updates. These diagnostics do not prove noise caused the v17 economic failure.

V18 asks whether **more precise paired simulator continuation targets**, with a predeclared longer but matched training budget, let the same dynamic bipartite actor exploit within-tick feedback. It adds no graph layer, attention, requester, physical action or reward shaping. C18 must beat the same-architecture frozen D18 and same-weight CF18 in paired H+tail cost; C18 beating a weaker 4-tape supervised C architecture L18 alone cannot establish the feedback claim. Sequential decoding, STOP, graph messages and imitation are prior art. The possible contribution is a useful empirically verified maintenance-demand/capacity coordination framework, not a new RL algorithm.

## Physics, probability and labels

Use exactly the v17 N5/K3 `n5k3_residual_a/b` configurations (seeds 74740000/74740001), threshold PM, automatic CM, skill/service/failure/restoration/cost law, H48+tail16 and every legal physical matching including WAIT. The canonical increasing-edge decoder, provisional within-tick tokens, STOP and forced termination, exact unique physical-matching path log probability and PPO sequence policy remain v17. Only the completed matching advances the simulator. The actor never receives future events or target costs. Entropy remains path-conditional, not full matching entropy. Greedy token selection need not maximize matching probability.

Reuse **only** the 508 independently audited v17 *training-label states and candidate matching sets* from `artifacts/full_matching_v17_labels_20261023T080338842451Z`. Their original four event tapes, branch costs, C16 behavior and S16 continuation are immutable. For each state and the same candidate set, generate 12 new independently namespaced event tapes; force each candidate at the selected state and follow the same fixed S16 continuation. Thus the 16-tape target contains the original four plus 12 new paired tapes, with exactly the same candidates, state distribution, continuation policy and temperature 20 as the 4-tape target. The 4-tape comparator L18 uses the old four-tape probabilities on exactly these states. The new tapes use `role_seed(v17_raw_seed, "v18-extra:<case>:<C16_root>:<tick>:<slot>")` for slots 4..15; verify no overlap with prior physical, bank or reserved v18 roles. No v17 DEV/held-out state or seed enters labels.

Label collection has a 3-hour timeout. Preserve every partial branch and failure in a fresh timestamped artifact. Independently replay state/candidate identities, all new event tapes, branch H/tail/total costs, target probabilities and source hashes. The 508-state bank must remain 273/235 by case. Before PPO, require the 16-tape split (first eight versus last eight) to show candidate-pair sign agreement at least 0.63 and best-candidate agreement at least 0.30 **in each case**; count ties explicitly. Failure is terminal for v18 and cannot be repaired by retuning these thresholds. These are label-reliability qualification checks, not evidence of learned policy gain.

## Arms, budgets and gates

C18/D18/I18/R18/B18/S18/G18 use the respective unchanged v16/v17 actor modules and masks; D18 freezes scoring context within a tick. CF18 loads each DEV-selected C18 state dict into D18 for evaluation. L18 uses C18's identical architecture, optimizer and 4-tape targets, isolating target precision at fixed training budget. P18 is graph PPO without pretraining; Q18 is flat always-request PPO. F1 risk matching and F2 six-tick rollout are contextual controls. All except declared Q18 have the same present-state information and threshold-admitted legal physical action set. C/D/I/R/B/S/G/L get 96 supervised epochs, batch 32, Adam 3e-4, gradient clip .5, identical rows and updates; C/D/I/R/B/S/G receive 16-tape labels, L receives four-tape. P/Q get no labels. This is a predeclared higher fitting budget after the train-only overfit check, not a claim that v18 differs from v17 solely in label precision. The C−L comparison isolates precision *within v18*.

Each case/arm/root receives 8,192 H PPO decisions, gamma .99, GAE .95, clip .2, entropy .01, rollout 256, minibatch 64, four epochs, Adam 3e-4, gradient clip .5 and one CPU Torch thread. Save deterministic checkpoints at 2,048/4,096/8,192 decisions and select minimum mean H+tail on 16 DEV physical seeds, earliest tie. Trial timeout 2 hours, no outcome-driven stopping. Report actor/critic parameters, label branches, pretraining updates, PPO decisions, actual scorer/graph passes, wall time, forecast transitions and episode inference latency.

Primary estimands are equal-case/equal-root paired C18−D18, C18−CF18 and C18−L18 H+tail cost; negative favors C18.
