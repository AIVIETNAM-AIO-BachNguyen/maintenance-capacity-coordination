# V20 recent-policy matching labels: audited findings

Status: **closed, no-go** on 2026-10-25 (Asia/Taipei). The protocol in `docs/on_policy_matching_value_v20_prelock.md` was locked before v20 implementation and outcomes. Scientific collection used clean, pushed source commit `0645bad8d855265f63c2baf5af2a3f67a702840a`. All training and evaluation data were generated by the unchanged simulator. No independent retraining or new TEST ran.

## Question and intervention

V19 retained matching-label rankings during PPO but did not improve physical cost. V20 replaced only the fixed 16-tape training label bank: it sampled fresh training-only states visited by selected C19/D19 policies and included their candidate matchings. C20/D20 kept the v19 actor, graph update/freeze distinction, PPO, 0.5 rehearsal weight, S16 continuation evaluator, threshold PM admission, legal matching/WAIT action set and physical simulator. C20−C19 measures the combined state/candidate-bank intervention. C20−D20 compares separately trained dynamic and frozen-context actors on that bank. CF20 uses C20 weights with within-tick context frozen at inference; C20−CF20 is the same-weight feedback comparison.

## Fresh held-out economic result

Each learned arm was evaluated on the same 32 fresh raw physical seeds in each case and training root. Differences are C20 minus comparator H48+tail16 cost, so negative favors C20. The 95% intervals resample raw physical seed clusters, preserving case/root/arm pairing. The three locked primary contrasts are:

| Contrast | Overall | Case a / b | Training roots 0 / 1 / 2 | Cluster 95% interval | Locked gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| C20 − C19, new bank | **+9.57** | +20.20 / −1.06 | +15.36 / −15.58 / +28.92 | [+1.94, +17.57] | ≤−10, both cases negative, upper <0: **fail**. |
| C20 − D20, trained feedback | **+18.68** | +24.63 / +12.74 | +29.58 / −0.39 / +26.86 | [+11.48, +25.89] | ≤−20, both cases negative, ≥2 roots negative, upper <0: **fail**. |
| C20 − CF20, same-weight feedback | **−7.37** | −4.14 / −10.60 | −15.73 / −2.05 / −4.33 | [−12.97, −2.05] | ≤−10, both cases negative, upper <0: **fail on magnitude**. |

C20's absolute pooled mean cost was **1584.27**, versus C19 **1574.70**, D20 **1565.58**, CF20 **1591.64**, B18 **1580.90**, S18 **1581.23**, G18 **1599.94** and D19 **1591.98**. C20−B18 was +3.37 and C20−S18 +3.04, failing their required negative signs; C20−G18 was −15.68. C20−F2 rollout was −3.73, satisfying the no-more-than-20-worse check, but this cannot rescue the failed primary gates. The independently audited `replication_go_decision.json` records `go: false`.

The same-weight C20−CF20 interval is entirely negative and both case means are negative. This is evidence of a modest physical benefit from refreshing context for *these C20 weights and this panel*. It misses the predeclared practical magnitude, and the C20 policy is worse than C19 and D20. The separately trained C20−D20 comparison includes training-path differences; it is not an isolated inference-time feedback effect. Changing the bank combines state support with candidate support, so this experiment does not identify which component caused the C20−C19 result.

C20 starts more service and waits less than C19/D20 on this panel, while its H failures and total cost are higher. These are descriptive associations, not a decomposition of the causal cost difference. Each new actor has 22,178 parameters and its critic 9,473. Mean trial wall time including PPO and DEV was 168.6 seconds for C20 versus 75.9 for D20; both used the same 8,192 physical training decisions per trial. Across six trials per arm, C20 made 47,905 training decoder forward passes, D20 48,115, and each arm evaluated roughly 780,000 rehearsal candidate paths. Equal environment decisions did not mean equal wall time.

## Decision and remaining uncertainty

V20 passed label reliability and independent replay audits, but the new training bank **increased** C20 held-out cost against matched C19 and C20 lost to D20. Same-weight feedback reduced cost by 7.37 with a negative cluster interval, short of the locked 10-unit practical threshold. Thus the current experiment does not establish a useful framework gain from recent-policy matching labels or within-tick graph feedback. The locked replication gate failed, so no independent retraining or TEST is eligible.

The unresolved question is why supervision on recent-policy states and choices helped neither the dynamic actor against its old-bank control nor the trained dynamic actor against frozen-context D20, despite a modest same-weight benefit. The current data do not distinguish label generalization, economic alignment, and the actor's ability to preserve scarce technician–machine alternatives. A follow-up would need a separately justified question and prelock; neither the v20 threshold nor its held-out panel may be tuned or reused for design selection.
