## Conditional timing, component, worker and joint choices

The audited [structural prevalence](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-stage2-20261025T110848Z/prevalence.csv), [Bank-B cost contrasts](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-stage2-20261025T110848Z/conditional_cost.csv), [joint quartets](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-stage2-20261025T110848Z/joint_interaction.csv) and [plots](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-stage2-20261025T110848Z/conditional_gaps.pdf) retain absent, zero and adverse motifs. T/C/W/J mark timing, component, worker and same-tick matching choices; A/R/Q mark assignment, release and explicit wait-for-a-faster-worker structures. Prevalence uses all 128 physical episodes per case/reference. Costs condition on up to 24 outcome-independently selected states and Bank-A-selected pairs; Bank B validates on distinct tapes.

Under risk trajectories, T/C/W appeared somewhere in every base, generalist and scale4 episode, but eligible W pairs existed in only 6/24, 17/24 and 15/24 selected states. Their Bank-B oriented T/C/W extreme gaps were 7.3/6.2/6.3, 8.1/14.6/12.3 and 9.2/15.3/18.7 units, respectively. Selection on A makes these descriptive and potentially optimistic. Homogeneous and surplus retain structurally legal worker alternatives yet have exactly zero W gap under interchangeable durations. Scarce has no W choice; restricted has no C or W pair under the locked same-target witness definition. Base has 18/24 J pairs; surplus has none. A positive local gap cannot be multiplied by episode prevalence to claim physical episode savings.

The independent [Bank-C confirmation motif table](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/confirmation_motifs.csv) applies the locked presence, `c_U`-scale and simultaneous adjusted-interval rules. In base, the corrective-reference T gap is 13.09 [0.91,25.28] and W is 26.90 [5.45,48.35]; risk-reference T/C/W intervals include zero. In generalist under risk, T is 11.58 [1.52,21.63], C 13.43 [5.31,21.55] and W 11.27 [1.59,20.95]. Restricted has no C/W pairs and its risk T interval includes zero. Memoryless, homogeneous and surplus have no confirmed conditional contrast under the locked rule, even though some legal choices remain. The full table reports both reference strata, absent motifs and all 13 cases.

Precision requires a separate qualification: the protocol calls an interval wider than `2c_U` insufficient. Each confirmed base/generalist interval above exceeds its 12-unit width limit. Thus the locked binary confirmation decision establishes direction and the preset mean floor, while effect magnitude in the proposed core cases remains insufficiently precise by the protocol's reporting criterion. No adaptive seed addition or case substitution was made. Some scarce risk contrasts meet that precision criterion, but scarce lacks W reachability and remains a prespecified stress case.

A joint quartet compares two simultaneous starts to each start alone and WAIT on common tapes. It measures conditional nonadditivity under the stated continuation; it does not isolate resource commitments from hazard and continuation effects. The Q witness has an explicit wait-for-faster-worker continuation. A root WAIT alone is never interpreted as that strategy.

## Present-information selectors, physical controls and horizon

The audited [Bank-B](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-stage2-20261025T110848Z/compute_frontier.csv) and [Bank-C](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/diagnostics-confirmation-20261025T130953Z/compute_frontier.csv) frontier tables report conditional cost relative to the risk root, R2/R4/R8 forecast transitions, selection latency and disjoint-half P-tape disagreement. In Bank C risk states, base R2/R4/R8 conditional gains are 0.77/9.21/16.36 units at 95/379/1,136 mean transitions; generalist values are −4.94/5.44/6.64 at 163/651/1,952 transitions. More computation can improve this *conditional* selector under risk continuation, with substantial extra time and unstable half-tape roots. These are not deployable episode-gain estimates or matched-compute comparisons with the cheap heuristics.

The separate [physical R2 diagnostic](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/physical-r2-stage2-20261025T111326Z/physical_R2_frontier.csv) found R2 worse than risk in all six cases on paired 64-seed episodes. The [independent physical confirmation table](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/physical_controls.csv) repeats that result across all 13 cases: risk minus R2 is −84.82 [−100.17,−69.47] in base and −140.81 [−156.39,−125.23] in generalist with multiplicity-adjusted intervals across 13 R2 comparisons. R2 averaged 1,527 and 3,895 forecast transitions per physical episode, versus zero for the heuristics. R2 uses the same current observation plus public model access; its failure does not prove that a learner is needed or that the setting lacks meaningful decisions.

The selected threshold5 control is stronger than risk on the independent physical bank: base total cost 856.2 versus risk 962.0, paired risk-minus-threshold5 105.8 [89.4,122.3]; generalist 766.0 versus 899.6, paired difference 133.7 [122.5,144.4]. Restricted threshold5 is 754.9 versus risk 876.8. These baseline parameters came from the separate selection bank. C1 therefore establishes neither episode-level headroom beyond the strongest control nor matched-compute value for a new representation. Conditional Bank-C costs use risk continuation and may change under threshold continuation.

The locked [sensitivity table](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/sensitivity.csv) and [plot](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/horizon_sensitivity.pdf) keep H and tail costs separate and normalize by machine-interval exposure. Under risk, base cost per machine-interval is 4.88 at H24+B8, 4.97 at H48+B0 and 5.10 at H96+B32; generalist is 4.45/4.54/4.67. Shortlife is approximately 5.70/5.73/5.71. Because H and B change together across the three settings, these are exposure-normalized sensitivity descriptions, not a pure causal tail-length effect. The fixed risk tail rule is unchanged wherever B is positive.

## Benchmark recommendation and next step

The [audited locked-role table](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/benchmark_roles.csv) recommends **base and generalist** as core *candidate populations*: T/C/W are structurally reachable, at least one Bank-C conditional contrast passes the prespecified sign and scale rule, and strong-control cost/compute measurements exist. Their qualifying effect intervals remain too wide for a precise magnitude claim. Restricted is an **unqualified core candidate** because C/W witnesses are absent under the locked definition; it is not replaced by an outcome-favorable case. Shortlife, scarce, scale4, pm_expensive, downtime_high and staggered remain **stress candidates**. Memoryless, homogeneous, surplus and longlife remain **negative/boundary controls**. No case was excluded for integrity failure.

One possible next method step, requiring a separate protocol and authorization, is a matched-compute direct-state versus demand–capacity representation pilot on base and generalist against the selected threshold control. It must specify a practical cost or amortized-compute criterion before opening new outcomes. C1 by itself does not show a learned-policy benefit, an attainable advantage over threshold5, or a deployment efficiency claim.

## Claim boundaries, correction and reproducibility

All intervals condition on the stated simulator hazard/duration/cost fixtures, initially healthy/free physical population, policy behavior, seed banks and horizons. They exclude hazard-model misspecification, monetary calibration and field external validity. Forecast tapes, branch roots and H ticks are nested within physical episodes, not independent experimental units. Scale claims stop at M=4,C=2,K=3. Centralized full-information and WAIT/corrective controls remain available. No learner, sealed TEST, branch, commit/push, SSH or rsync was used.

The earlier automatic approval review rejected the attempted full Stage 1 scientific launch because the authorization then appeared only in an attached goal file, which that review did not accept as sufficient authority. It delayed scientific collection and analysis; completed engineering tests and bounded smoke were unaffected. The user's later explicit in-chat authorization permitted the lab campaign. No approval mechanism was bypassed.

The first confirmation report invocation failed before output because its input checker indexed `manifest["stage"]` on an economic manifest that exposes `bank_stage`. The report reader was mechanically corrected to `manifest.get("stage")` after the preconfirmation source lock; no configuration, seed, estimator, threshold or decision rule changed. The frozen lock retains the original hash; the [postlock erratum](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/postlock_analysis_erratum.json) records both hashes and the mechanical fix, and [confirmation.json](../artifacts/jcst_analysis/confirmation-report-20261025T131045Z/confirmation.json) records the corrected report-code hash. Both diagnostic and decision arithmetic auditors passed afterward. An earlier confirmation-diagnostic analysis directory had an incorrect future UTC label; it was preserved, and the correctly timestamped, audited `diagnostics-confirmation-20261025T130953Z` is the cited artifact.
