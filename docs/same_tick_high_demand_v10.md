## Why this setting and what stays fixed

The original N4/K2 decoder, complete-matching PPO controls and five frozen V14 checkpoints all require N4/K2. V8's two original skill graphs had scarce residual choices on C8-visited states; v9's two-case qualification failed its joint threshold and therefore did not train. A new simulation sensitivity study can ask whether the unchanged contextual-update mechanism helps when degradation produces demand earlier, without conditioning case selection on a v9 winner. Fix **both** pre-existing heterogeneous skill graphs, reciprocal and specialist/generalist, at their pre-existing scale-1 service durations. Set `failure_age=3` for **both**. The underlying simulator already permits failure-age values 3, 4 and 5 in its `training_config`; here 3 represents a higher-demand degradation scenario. The only changes from each family scale-1 configuration are that parameter and the case name. In particular N4/K2, failure Bernoulli mechanism and probability .45, age progression, restoration .75, PM/CM lifecycle, non-preemptive service, costs, threshold requester (`failure_age - 1`), H=48, tail=16 and simultaneous final matching execution remain as implemented. The parameter shift and its effect on demand are disclosed; this is a simulated stress setting, not factory calibration or an alteration chosen to guarantee a learner win. Do not modify any physical transition, inject jobs, force requests, add fees/shutdowns, hide state, or use future tape inputs.

Cases fixed now: `reciprocal_age3_scale1` and `specialist_generalist_age3_scale1`. Do not remove either case or replace one after training, DEV or held-out outcomes. No pre-pilot scientific opportunity screening will choose a case. Report the realized frequency of root graphs with multiple possible second edges descriptively on held-out policy-visited states.

## Hypothesis, actors and probability

Hypothesis: with earlier maintenance demand, recomputing provisional remaining-machine, remaining-technician and selected-edge context after a first within-tick pair improves H+tail economic cost compared with the identical network freezing that context. The primary estimand is equal-case, equal-training-root, paired-physical-seed mean **C10 minus D10** H+tail cost, negative favorable. This is a test of the existing v8 mechanism under a newly specified simulator parameter regime, not a claim of new RL-algorithm novelty.

C10/D10/B10/S10/G10 use exactly the source architectures and PPO hyperparameters of v8. C10 is contextual sequential; D10 has identical modules, weights at initialization, present-state information and root logits, but freezes the initial context within the tick while feasibility updates; B10 is direct-feature sequential; S10 is structured complete-action; G10 is flat full-information complete-action. All scratch arms have the same legal final matching/WAIT set and present-state input. Frozen V14 (five historical roots), threshold + risk matching F1 and threshold + six-tick rollout F2 run on the same physical panels as contextual references; they are not scratch-budget matched. The V14 checkpoints were selected on other configurations and may transfer poorly to this shifted one; disclose that limitation. No binary requester is learned.

For the three sequential actors, the PPO random variable is the ordered internal sequence of feasible edge tokens and STOP, with one H physical reward assigned to the whole sequence. Sum exact conditional sequence log probabilities for the old/new PPO ratio. Do not call a sequence probability a marginal matching probability, or sampled-path sequence entropy matching entropy. Multiple sequences can yield the same physical matching. Deterministic tokenwise greedy decoding need not maximize physical matching probability. STOP at root is WAIT, after one edge is a singleton, and exhausted feasible edges force termination without an additional stochastic token. The final matching is executed once; no decoder step advances time, event tape, cost or physical commitment.
