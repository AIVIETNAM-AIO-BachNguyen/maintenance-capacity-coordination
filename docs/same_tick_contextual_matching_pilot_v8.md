# Same-tick contextual matching v8 — prelocked pilot

Status: LOCKED BEFORE V8 LEARNING OUTCOMES, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). The formulation is in `same_tick_contextual_matching_proposal_v8.md`. This protocol supersedes neither v1–v7 gates nor their verdicts. No v8 TEST is authorized.

## Hypothesis, contract, estimand

On the unchanged N4/K2 `reciprocal_scale2` and `specialist_generalist_scale2` configurations, recomputing remaining-demand, remaining-capacity and provisional-selected-edge context after each within-tick pair improves finite-budget PPO H+tail economic cost. The primary estimand is the equal-case, equal-training-root, paired-physical-seed mean of **C8 minus D8** H+tail cost; negative favors feedback. Secondary attribution contrasts are C8–B8, C8–S8 and C8–G8. The fixed threshold admits PM; CM remains automatic. Every arm has the same legal partial-matching/WAIT physical actions and full current information. One final matching is executed per H tick. No internal decoder step advances physical time, reads future events, receives a physical reward, or changes service state. STOP initially is WAIT; STOP after one edge yields a singleton; exhausted edges force termination. No maximum-cardinality constraint.

| G8 | V3 G3 flat full-information PPO architecture, trained afresh | Flat control |
| V14/F1/F2 | Frozen V14, risk matching, inherited six-tick rollout | Same-panel strong references, not sample-matched scratch actors |

For C8/D8/B8, the PPO random variable is the **ordered sequence**, including terminal STOP where available. Its exact conditional log probabilities sum. The old/new PPO ratio is the ratio of probabilities of that sampled sequence, even when several sequences map to one physical matching. No sequence log probability is called matching marginal probability. Entropy regularization uses the sum of conditional **sequence** entropies along the sampled path; deterministic inference greedily picks a token at each step. It may differ from the most probable physical matching. S8/G8 retain one masked categorical over complete matchings. All use the same present-state critic architecture and physical rewards. Report sequence-length/order frequencies, pair multiplicity, entropy, decoder forward passes and inference/training wall time; do not infer sample equivalence from equal H ticks alone.

## Analysis, gate, progression

Primary metric: paired selected-policy H+tail economic cost. Report case/root means and a 95% percentile interval from 5,000 resamples of the **raw physical seed ID as one cluster across cases, arms and roots**, conditional on trained roots. Report training-root variation separately. Secondary: H availability/failures, PM/CM queue and backlog, service starts, WAIT, matching cardinality, generalist allocation, learning curves/area, valid-action rates, inference latency, actor/critic count, H/tail transitions, decoder forward passes, forecast transitions and full wall time. Mechanism: on the same frozen physical states, count and characterize C8/D8 second-step score/order changes; compare completed matching and H+tail outcomes. Changed actions alone are not a benefit. Do not sum local score/regret differences into episode gain.

The independent-training-replication gate requires C8–D8 overall mean ≤ −20 H+tail units, both case means < 0, at least two of three training-root means < 0, and clustered interval upper endpoint < 0. It additionally requires C8–B8 overall < 0 and C8–G8 overall < 0, so representation and flat control are not ignored. All thresholds/signs were selected before v8 outcomes. If the gate fails, stop v8 without replication or TEST. If it passes, independently retrain all five scratch arms on the reserved new roots with the identical code/protocol and evaluate on its fresh physical panel. Replication must retain C8–D8 and C8–G8 direction, both case directions and interval upper endpoint < 0 before requesting separate TEST authorization. No TEST entry point will run without that authorization. Frozen V14/F1/F2 are strong cost references; a C8 loss to them limits practical claims even if the attribution gate passes.
