# Same-tick simulation regime v9 — locked before new-panel outcomes

Date: 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). This is one conditional experiment, not a revision of the v8 gate. RL training and evaluation data are simulator trajectories. No operational dataset is required. No new TEST is authorized.

## Hypothesis and operating contract

V8's held-out C8-visited root graphs offered multiple second assignments in only 27/9,216 H states. This v9 experiment asks whether the **same** within-tick contextual update has an economically useful effect in two already-defined N4/K2 simulator configurations with shorter service commitments: `interchangeable_scale1` and `reciprocal_scale1` from `family_configs("full")`. These cases are fixed here, before looking at their new-panel outcomes. The two configurations preserve failure law, PM/CM lifecycle, restoration, costs, 48 H decisions, 16-tick tail, full present-state information, threshold PM requests, non-preemptive service and simultaneous execution of one completed matching per tick. They change only the existing service-duration/compatibility matrix. Do not alter the physics, force requests, inject jobs, gate actions, or use a future event tape in policy inputs. The simulator settings are research assumptions, not a claim of factory calibration.

## Stage 1: one structural qualification

Use a frozen deterministic threshold-request + risk-matching controller on raw physical seeds `73501000..73501031`, both cases, 48 H ticks/episode and the inherited tail. At each H tick, after threshold admission and before execution, enumerate all legal first machine–technician edges. A root state qualifies if **at least one** legal first edge leaves **at least two** compatible, idle, distinct-machine/distinct-technician second edges. Count qualifying states and total H states by case. This measures opportunity, not outcome value or learned-policy behavior. Log every tick's seed, selected risk matching, legal root edges and qualifying first edges. Independently replay the complete trajectories and H+tail accounting before reading the gate. No qualification seed can be reused for training, DEV or held-out evaluation.

The locked gate requires at least 3% qualifying H states **in each case** (at least 47 of 1,536 states per case). If either case fails, stop v9 before PPO training; report the no-go and do not adjust cases, threshold or gate on these outcomes. A pass licenses only the conditional pilot below, not a mechanistic or economic claim.

## Stage 2: conditional same-physics PPO pilot

Use unchanged v8 actor/critic architectures and ordered-sequence PPO formulation, renamed C9/D9/B9/S9/G9 only for protocol identity. C9 recomputes provisional partial-matching context after each selected edge; D9 has identical parameters, input and initial score but freezes that context, while both update feasibility. B9 is direct-feature sequential; S9 structured complete-matching PPO; G9 flat full-information complete-matching PPO. All five have the same current-state information, feasible physical matching/WAIT set, physical reward and simulator tape. Frozen V14, threshold + risk matching and threshold + six-tick rollout F2 are same-panel strong references, not sample-matched scratch learners. No new architecture, loss or action set is introduced.

For sequential arms the sampled **ordered internal sequence** is PPO's random variable; exact conditional log probabilities sum, and the ratio is for that sequence. Multiple sequences may yield the same physical matching. Sampled-path sequence entropy is not matching entropy. Deterministic inference is tokenwise greedy; it need not produce the most probable physical matching. A STOP at root is WAIT, STOP after an edge is a partial matching, and physical time/reward advances once after a final matching. Forced termination is not an extra stochastic token. The v8 mask, normalization, same-weight C/D and adapter tests must still pass on both v9 configurations.

Train roots `73510000..73510002`; DEV seeds `73511000..73511015`; held-out physical seeds `73512000..73512031`; cluster-bootstrap RNG `73513000`. Conditional independent retraining, only if eligible: roots `73520000..73520002`, DEV `73521000..73521015`, evaluation `73522000..73522031`, bootstrap `73523000`. Reserve unopened TEST `73530000..73530031`. Engineering smoke uses `73500000..73500002`. Derive training episode resets with `role_seed(root, "same_tick_v9:<case>:<episode>")` and the adapter's unchanged physical-tape derivation. Check raw and derived role separation from previous experiments.

Each case/arm/root gets 8,192 H physical decisions with 256-step PPO rollouts, minibatch 64, four epochs, Adam 3e-4, gamma .99, GAE .95, clip .2, entropy coefficient .01, gradient clip .5, CPU/one Torch thread. Evaluate deterministic checkpoints on DEV at 2,048/4,096/8,192 decisions; choose minimum mean H+tail, ties to earlier milestone. Two-hour trial timeout, preserve partials and failures. Use tqdm. Record H transitions, decoder scorer calls, sequence lengths/order, entropy, parameter count, training/inference wall time and forecast transitions. Count scorer calls as one root call plus one after each selected edge, including a forced-end call; do not reuse the v8 telemetry field as scorer calls.

Primary estimand: equal-case, equal-training-root and equal-paired-physical-seed mean C9−D9 H+tail economic cost, negative favorable. Report by case/root; use 5,000 raw-physical-seed-cluster bootstrap resamples across all case/arm/root rows, conditioning on trained roots, and separately report root variation. Secondary: C9−B9, C9−S9, C9−G9; V14/F1/F2 reference costs; H availability/failures, PM/CM queues, service starts, WAIT, matching cardinality, generalist allocation and compute. Same-weight context freezing on C9-visited states may diagnose token/rank changes but cannot by itself prove episode benefit.

The locked gate for **independent training replication** is C9−D9 overall ≤−20 H+tail, both case means negative, at least two of three root means negative, physical-cluster interval upper endpoint <0, plus C9−B9 and C9−G9 overall negative. If any condition fails, stop without replication or TEST. If it passes, retrain all five scratch arms on the reserved fresh roots with unchanged code/protocol and require C9−D9 and C9−G9 negative, both case directions negative and interval upper endpoint <0 before separately requesting TEST authorization. A C9 loss to strong F2 limits practical claims even if attribution gates pass. Do not lower any gate or choose cases/checkpoints on held-out outcomes.
