# Scaled same-tick coordination v11 — engineering design before research outcomes

Date: 2026-10-23 (Asia/Taipei). This document opens one engineering track toward a new prelocked simulator experiment; it does not open a scientific seed panel or revise any v1–v10 verdict. RL data will be simulator rollouts. No TEST is authorized.

## Why scale is a distinct question

Across audited v8 and v10 C-visited N4/K2 panels, only 27/9,216 and 86/9,216 H states respectively had a root first edge leaving at least two feasible second edges. A K2 decoder cannot test conditional reranking frequently when only one technician remains after the first pair. Merely retuning a pooled context network on those panels is unlikely to resolve this structural exposure problem. The inherited physical `DispatchConfig`, request lifecycle, `legal_matchings`, `catalogue`, risk and rollout controls already support N/K greater than 4/2; the research adapter and learned actors do not. Implementing a 5-machine/3-technician pilot makes the hypothesis testable in a richer but still finite action space, without introducing a new physical action or a new RL algorithm.

Use the pre-existing `family_config("n5_k3_sparse", seed, "full")` generator, with **fixed configuration seeds 73740000 and 73740001** and horizon replaced by 64 to preserve H48+tail16. These two independently fixed skill/duration/restoration matrices are not chosen from outcome screening. The family already defines failure age 3, failure probability .60, non-preemptive service durations 2–4 (zero denotes incompatibility), and the inherited costs. No fees, forced shutdowns, hidden information, altered failure transition, new request rule or injected workload. The two generated configs and their hashes must be frozen in the eventual protocol before scientific outcomes. Do not drop one based on smoke, DEV or held-out cost.

## Interfaces and formulation

Create a versioned N5/K3 adapter over the unchanged `RequestEnv`: threshold PM admission; automatic CM; legal partial matching including WAIT; one physical transition after a completed matching; H48 and inherited 16-tick risk tail. Present-state observations and masks generalize to N5/K3, while future events remain inaccessible to actors. `catalogue(5,3)` has 136 physical actions. Exact environment matching and H+tail accounting must equal the inherited kernel when forced to the same matching.

Generalize the v8 sequential coordinator to 15 edge tokens plus STOP, at most four internal tokens, shared machine/worker encoders, initial/frozen context D and recomputed partial-matching context C. A direct-feature sequential B separates demand representation. A structured complete-action S and flat full-information G both score the full 136-action set. An always-request + learned full-information Q is an additional contextual control: its admission set differs, so it is not an architecture-attribution comparator. Risk matching and six-tick rollout F2 are same-panel known-model references. The historical frozen V14 actor is hard-coded for a 56-coordinate N4/K2 observation and 21 N4/K2 actions; it cannot fairly score N5/K3 matchings. Do not label a projected V14 as a same-action strong control. Retain the published frozen V14 evidence only as an external N4/K2 reference, and include a newly trained full-information N5/K3 strong baseline in the new panel. Document this scope limit explicitly in the eventual protocol.

Sequential PPO continues to treat the sampled **ordered internal sequence** as the stochastic action; physical reward belongs to one completed matching transition. Exact masked conditional log probabilities determine PPO ratios. Multiple orders can map to the same matching; sequence entropy is not matching entropy. Deterministic decoding is tokenwise greedy, not necessarily the maximum-probability physical matching. Feasibility removes selected machines/technicians; STOP yields WAIT or a partial matching. No decoder step advances the simulator.
