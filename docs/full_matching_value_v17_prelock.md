# V17 complete-matching value coverage: locked successor

Status: locked after the audited v16 no-go and descriptive label-support check, **before any v17 implementation, smoke, labels, train/DEV or held-out outcome**. V1–v16 verdicts and their gates remain closed. This is the one current research experiment. Simulator trajectories are the only training data. No new TEST is authorized.

## Hypothesis and limits

V16 repaired the raw-input path and recomputed machine↔technician messages, but C16−CF16 did not show a useful same-weight economic gain. Its inherited v15 supervision compared only exact two-edge matchings at one C12-visited tick in 8–39. A descriptive audit of C16-visited held-out decisions found 1,628 second-token opportunities, including 571 outside that window, 515 second-token STOPs and 38 third tokens. Every C16 first-edge type appeared somewhere in the label bank, so the issue is **target/state coverage**, not a proven first-edge support failure. This diagnostic does not establish why v16 failed.

V17 asks whether **supervising the exact complete physical matching**, including WAIT, singleton STOP and cardinality-two/three choices on fresh C16-visited simulator states across H, lets the unchanged dynamic bipartite encoder learn a practically useful feedback policy. If C17 does not beat same-architecture frozen D17 and same-weight CF17 on fresh paired H+tail episodes, the feedback contribution remains unsupported. A gain over flat PPO alone does not suffice. Sequential matching, shared embeddings, graph messages, STOP and imitation/value pretraining are prior art; the proposed contribution is an empirically useful coordination framework, not a new RL optimizer. Do not add attention, a second message pass, a requester policy, reward shaping or a new physical action.

## Physical contract, actors and probability

Reuse exactly the two fixed `n5k3_residual_a/b` configurations (config seeds 74740000/74740001), N5/K3, H48+tail16, threshold PM, automatic CM, existing skill/service/restoration/failure/cost law, and all 136 legal physical matchings including WAIT. Decoder choices remain provisional and cause no physical transition/cost/tape advance until one final matching executes. C17/D17/I17/R17/B17/S17/G17/P17/Q17 have exactly the respective v16 modules, widths, masks and PPO formulation; O17 is an additional C16-architecture old-label comparator. D17 freezes scoring context after the root with the same modules and parameters as C17; CF17 loads each DEV-selected C17 state dict into D17 at evaluation. I17 pools all entities without graph messages; R17 is old feasible-edge, B17 direct sequential, S17 structured complete-matching, G17 flat full-information, P17 graph PPO-only, Q17 flat always-request. C/D/I/R/B/S/G receive the new matching-value labels. O17 receives the unchanged audited v15 exact-two-edge bank under its original 64-epoch pretraining. P/Q receive no labels. C−O measures the training-package contrast, not pure coverage attribution because label records/epochs and compute differ; report them.

For each label state, give every candidate physical matching its **unique increasing-edge canonical sequence**; STOP is explicit whenever another edge is feasible, and forced termination is recorded when none remains. The physical matching probability is the exact product of that sequence's conditional token probabilities. Supervised listwise loss uses the softmax of those *exact matching log probabilities restricted to the preselected candidate subset*. It is a conditional ranking surrogate, not a claim that the subset softmax equals the full policy distribution. S/G use their full-matching categorical logits restricted to the same subset. PPO remains the v16 exact canonical sequence policy (one return per executed matching); log probability/ratio are over its unique physical-matching path, and entropy reports path conditionals rather than full matching entropy. Greedy token decoding need not return the maximum-probability matching. No future event or target cost enters inference.

## Fresh simulator label bank

Use only DEV-selected, audited C16 checkpoints from v16's three training roots as fixed **behavior policies** for state collection, and the corresponding DEV-selected S16 checkpoints as fixed post-branch continuations. Checkpoint hashes are locked from v16's selection files and verified at runtime. No v16 held-out state or seed enters v17 labels. For each case, C16 behavior root and raw label seed 79701000..79701047, execute one deterministic 48-tick episode. Within windows 0..23 and 24..47, collect all ticks whose root feasible graph has at least one first edge with two higher-index nonconflicting second edges; choose one qualifying tick per nonempty window using a deterministic role seed independent of future tape. Record missing windows. This gives at most 576 states, with temporal coverage; training may proceed only if at least 300 states total and 120 per case are labelled. A coverage failure is terminal for this locked pilot and must be reported without loosening criteria.

At each chosen state, compute the current legal physical matching set under threshold admission. Before any future event is sampled, form up to eight unique candidates in this exact order: WAIT; current C16 greedy matching; the same checkpoint with frozen D16 behavior; same-root S16 greedy matching; then one uniformly hashed remaining legal matching at each available cardinality 1, 2 and 3; then fill to eight by a uniformly hashed order over remaining legal matchings. Duplicate recommendations are removed; if fewer than eight legal actions exist, use all. Candidate sampling and state selection are independent of continuation costs. Record the complete candidate list, all inclusion reasons and canonical sequences. This fixed subset includes actual policy alternatives and action/cardinality controls but is not an exhaustive 136-action oracle.

For every candidate, force that **one** matching at the selected state, then follow the same fixed S16 continuation through H+tail. Reuse four independent event tapes across candidates at that state, each derived from a separate v17 bank seed. Cost target is remaining H+tail objective after the state, with the common accrued prefix subtracted; preserve H and tail components. Target probabilities are softmax of negative four-tape mean cost with temperature 20, matching v15. The label is a continuation-specific ranking, not an optimal Q value. No branch tape enters actor observations or current legal masks. A two-hour label-run timeout is fixed. Preserve failed/partial artifacts.

New label pretraining uses 32 epochs, batch 32 candidate sets, Adam 3e-4, gradient clipping .5 and a fresh optimizer for PPO. All seven new-label arms get identical rows and epochs. O17 receives exactly the audited v15 bank with its original 64 epochs/batch64. Validate exact unique path/probability normalization, candidate restriction, STOP/forced ends, no duplicate physical matching, no future leakage, identical branch tapes, and branch-to-physical-kernel equivalence before scientific labels.
