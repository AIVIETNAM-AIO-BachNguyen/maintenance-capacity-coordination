**Evidence classes.** The V13 conditional value qualification and V14–V21 paired physical results are scientific evidence within their locked populations and budgets. Ubuntu smokes and unit tests are engineering validation. V17–V21 training-label fit, information-path and same-state action probes are descriptive post-hoc diagnostics, not independent gains. Score–decoder alignment, off-bank ranking and economic value of a decoder change are currently untested hypotheses. Physical seeds sharing a tape are paired clusters; training roots are a separate variation source.
