# Joint demand–capacity matching: v6 learner proposal

Status: separate mechanism pilot proposed after the audited v5 bounded gate, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). It does not amend v1–v5 outcomes or simulator semantics.

## Operational question and evidence

A full legal matching may start two non-preemptive services at once. In reciprocal N4/K2, the two starts jointly occupy technicians and alter future failure/wait exposure. V5's fresh independent-bank diagnostic found that replacing F2's complete-matching assessment with the sum of singleton effects lost +2.91 finite six-tick forecast cost on two-start-eligible states (151/768 H states; episode-cluster interval [+1.53,+4.65]). Specialist/generalist interaction was much smaller. This is a specific representation opportunity *relative to an additive decomposition*, not proof that a learner can beat F2 or improve realized H+tail cost.

## Minimal learned mechanism

On unchanged fixed-threshold requests and complete masked matchings, a shared machine encoder represents present health/request need and known per-technician skill, duration and restoration; a shared worker encoder represents availability and remaining commitments. A shared edge scorer produces an uncalibrated machine–technician service score. The proposed J6 coordinator scores a legal matching by summing selected edge scores **plus one explicit two-edge interaction score** if two jobs start together. WAIT scores zero. The interaction network receives both selected edge representations, so it can model nonadditive combined service commitments. A masked deterministic argmax executes the complete matching; there is no independent machine action or calibrated economic machine value. Physical transitions, failure law, CM, economic costs and tail stay inherited. No future realized events enter the actor.

The matched A6 ablation removes the two-edge term while widening its edge network to near-match active parameter count; it sees the same present observations and legal mask. Flat G6 sees the same raw full-information actor inputs and legal mask with a near-matched parameter count, and may learn arbitrary interactions. All three use **identical** F2 teacher states, full root-score targets, regression budget and DEV selection rule. The target is each legal root's finite F2 forecast **benefit relative to WAIT**, divided by a fixed scale; it is not true action value or a new economic reward. This full-score supervision is the same for every arm. It differs from v4's hard-action imitation because the tested mechanism is the nonadditive *relative root score*, not merely teacher action agreement. V4's locked source, held-out, replication and TEST remain closed.

F2 is the known-model full-matching reference and a same-panel control, not an optimum. Frozen V14 and risk matching are same-panel cost controls. Frozen selected threshold + learned-coordinator G and always-request + learned-coordinator D checkpoints are also replayed on the same physical panel as contextual controls; their earlier PPO training resources differ, so they are not equal-budget attribution arms. Historical v3 PPO results are context only. The primary same-information/action/budget attribution is J6 versus A6 and G6. Success requires realized H+tail differences and improved held-out pair-interaction score fidelity; the v5 forecast result alone cannot establish it. If these fail, retain the negative result rather than adjusting loss, architecture, target scale, seeds or gate on held-out data.
