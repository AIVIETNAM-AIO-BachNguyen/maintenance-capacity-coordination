## Three separate questions

| Question | Evidence target | What failure means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S — Quality of the setting** | Reachable timing and assignment decisions under fixed reference trajectories, plus independently validated conditional economic differences. No C21 or learned selector. | The named mechanism is not established *in these cases, reference populations, horizon, continuation and budget*. It does not prove the physical setting universally useless. |
| **I — Selectability from present information** | A fixed known-model forecast rule, using only current state/config and fresh simulated scenarios, ranks/chooses among legal actions; an independent future bank verifies its ranking and advantage over F1 risk matching. | This selector at its stated compute budget is unqualified. S can remain positive. Do not infer that no learner or other present-information controller could exploit the setting. |
| **F — Headroom beyond F2** | Independent-bank comparison of F2's root matching with the best action selected by the model forecast, and separately with the low-budget selector. | No superiority over this **six-tick F2 root** has been demonstrated. This is never a veto on S or I; a failed P-selector-versus-F2 contrast cannot be used to reject DC-F0/1. |

All branch costs use the same counterfactual: freeze an admitted present state at tick `t`, force one legal complete matching, then use threshold admission plus `risk_matching` on each subsequent interval through tick 63. Actual future events are never input to candidate selection. Branch gaps are conditional on this continuation, not an H48+tail16 whole-policy gain. Every reference arm receives the same current-state/config information; forecast computation, inference time and wall time are disclosed. A full-information centralized policy is an admissible solution, not an oracle by definition.

## Structural scope: timing, assignment and overlap

For each admitted H state enumerate the **entire** legal complete-matching set `𝒜(s)`, including WAIT. Record three witness indicators before seeing any branch cost:

- **T (timing):** an unstarted queued PM machine `m` and free compatible worker `j` admit two matchings `A` and `A∪{(m,j)}` with all other pairs identical. One starts PM now; the other leaves that PM running/at risk for this interval. A lone PM can satisfy T even without worker competition.
- **A (contested-worker allocation):** two distinct queued machines `m,n` can use the same free worker `j`; two legal matchings with identical other pairs assign `j` to `m` versus `n`. This is an allocation contrast, **not automatically a binding capacity bottleneck**: another worker might serve the displaced machine. It may change both assignment and service timing; it is not a pure technician-skill estimand.
- **B (binding capacity subset of A):** **no legal matching in the full state catalogue** can start both `m` and `n` in this tick, even after rearranging every other assignment. Compute this from the free-worker compatibility graph by exact matching, rather than inferring it from a shared edge or from the number of free workers. B identifies a state-level same-tick tradeoff in *which machine starts*; report its frequency and economic gap separately from A. A merely conditional bottleneck created by fixing unrelated pairs does not count as B.
- **J (binding joint timing–assignment witness):** a B pair has at least one queued, healthy PM member, and the alternatives start that PM in one matching and defer it in the other. Keep all other pairs fixed. `T∩A` alone is insufficient because its witnesses might be unrelated or nonbinding. Report J prevalence and its own validated contrast; it is not inherited from an unrelated T or A extremum.

A witness proves *opportunity*, not economic importance. T and A at different ticks establish two separately useful decision types, not their interaction. B establishes binding allocation, and only an economically validated J supports the **same-tick PM timing–assignment** claim. Sparse J does not refute possible multi-period coupling, but this protocol does not validate a multi-period interaction claim; that would need a separately specified trajectory-level contrast. Record worker occupancy, service-duration and cardinality strata, the full legal matching count, and whether alternative workers allow both machines to start. The V22 equal-edge-count/different-residual-capacity motif is narrower and is neither required nor re-gated here.

## Sampling, contrasts and catalogue coverage

- Fix `n5k3_residual_a/b` and raw physical seeds **84901000..84901023** in both cases. Run threshold admission with F1 `risk_matching` and F2 six-tick `rollout` separately on the same seed IDs: 96 trajectories, 4,608 H ticks. No case, mask or reference is selected from qualification outcomes. Count T/A/B/J on **all** H ticks. In each episode and each of T/A/B/J, choose at most one eligible tick by the smallest deterministic role hash of `(seed,case,reference,tick,stratum)`; a state selected by multiple strata is simulated once. The hash uses legality only, before branch costs, learner scores or future tapes. There is no replacement if absent. Thus at most 384 distinct labeled states. Exposure is a population-frequency estimate for these reference trajectories; labeled-state gaps are conditional on selected opportunities.
- At every selected state, enumerate the **entire** legal complete-matching set `𝒜(s)`, including WAIT. With N5/K3, the maximum is `1+15+60+60=136`. Stop as `INVALID` if there are duplicates, illegal actions or more than 136. For each present T/A/B/J stratum, form all witness pairs from the legal set **before viewing costs**. Partition them into physical motif families: T by `(m,j)`; A/B/J by the unordered machines `{m,n}` and contested worker `j`. For a cost-independent *sentinel*, use seeded priorities to choose a motif family uniformly, then a matching background/pair uniformly within that family. This avoids giving a motif more sentinel weight merely because it has more catalogue realizations, and is independent of matching enumeration order and forecast value. Report motif and pair counts, matching-cardinality and worker/compatibility strata, and sentinel results even if they are small.
- **Bank A** scores every legal matching on eight common forecast tapes and chooses the largest absolute forecast-gap witness pair **separately** in each available T/A/B/J stratum, oriented `(worse_A,better_A)`. It also chooses the minimum-cost root over the complete catalogue. Bank A therefore cannot omit a favorable action because an index-based top-k bank excluded it. It can still suffer a *many-candidates winner's curse*: more witness pairs give more chances for a noisy maximum. Independent Bank B validates the frozen choices, and report selected-versus-sentinel gaps against catalogue size; never describe the Bank-A maximum as the true global value gap. Exact forecast ties use a recorded seeded hash of canonical action IDs, not lowest machine/worker index. For a relabeling invariance test, transport the sampled tie/sentinel priorities with the physical entities and relabel the full state/config, action and tape consistently; structural counts, selected physical actions and costs must then correspond. A fresh hash of changed numeric IDs is a different randomization, not an invariance test. Failure of the transported-priority test is `INVALID`.
- **Bank P**, independent of Bank A, scores every legal matching on four common forecast tapes from the current state/config and selects its minimum-cost root with the same tie convention. Its two disjoint two-tape halves each select a root. P is a high-compute, present-information diagnostic, not a trained policy or access to actual future events.
- Freeze a compact **Bank B** action set containing Bank-A overall best/worst, endpoints of each available T/A/B/J extreme pair and cost-independent sentinel pair, WAIT, F1 and F2 roots, Bank-P root and both Bank-P half-roots; deduplicate by canonical action ID. The cap is 24 actions. Bank B evaluates each on 16 paired common tapes independent of the actual trajectory, A and P. Its validation is unbiased *conditional on the frozen candidate selection*, but says nothing about unvalidated catalogue actions. In particular, Bank A and P are forecast selectors at their disclosed budgets, not clairvoyant optima.
- Role-separated seeds `dcf0_q2:A`, `dcf0_q2:P`, `dcf0_q2:B` depend on raw physical seed, case, reference, tick and tape index, never the stratum; shared B/J states reuse the same tapes. Record exact registry and hashes. No Bank-B result can alter the catalogue, state sampling, selectors, F2 settings, continuation, thresholds or budgets. Prior held-out/TEST outcomes cannot set these choices.

The ceiling is `384 × (136×8 + 136×4 + 24×16) = 774,144` forced branches of at most 64 intervals; duplicates reduce actual work. Reference H decisions and F2 forecast transitions are extra and logged. This is a cap, not an adaptive target. Collection timeout **16 hours**, independent audit timeout **8 hours**; a cap/timeout produces `INCOMPLETE` and no gate decision. If engineering smoke predicts the cap cannot be met, amend the *unrun* protocol before any scientific collection rather than silently subsample after seeing outcomes. No collection is authorized here.

## Estimands, Monte Carlo uncertainty and decisions

For state `s`, `D_B(a,b|s)` is the mean over 16 **paired** Bank-B tapes of `cost(a)−cost(b)` after forcing the root action and using the fixed risk-matching continuation. For each T/A/B/J stratum report the Bank-B gap of its frozen Bank-A extreme pair and its hash-selected sentinel pair; orient both using Bank A alone. A positive extreme gap is an independently validated **available contrast under this candidate-selection procedure**, not average action quality or achievable episode gain. Sentinel gaps and pair-count strata show how much the conclusion depends on seeking an extreme in a large catalogue. Do not reorient from Bank B or drop negative rows. Report F1/F2 and Bank-P root costs on the same Bank-B tapes, plus H/tail components and forecast transitions, inference and wall time.

Separate three conclusions:

1. **S, setting quality:** reachability on complete reference trajectories, then independently validated T and A consequence. Report B and J as *nested, stronger scopes*. T plus A at different states does not establish an interaction. A binding-capacity claim needs B; a direct same-tick PM timing–assignment claim needs J's own exposure and economic gate.
2. **I, present-information selection:** Bank P's Bank-B improvement over F1 risk root and its directional accuracy on the frozen Bank-A T/A/B/J pairs. A selector failure is about this rule, bank size and continuation; it cannot negate S. Even I success is not a learned-policy result.
3. **F, headroom beyond F2:** separately `D_B(F2,best_A|s)` and `D_B(F2,best_P|s)`. The former is a higher-budget forecast opportunity; the latter is a four-tape present-state selector contrast. A positive conditional contrast is neither fair algorithm superiority at unmatched compute nor a whole-episode advantage. F failure cannot negate S or I.

Exposure averages the 48 tick indicators **within each episode**, then uses equal case → reference-policy → episode weighting. Conditional T/A/B/J gaps average over episodes with that selected witness, with equal case/reference weighting; report qualification rates and gaps together so a rare witness cannot appear population-wide. Root-versus-F1/F2 contrasts use each **unique selected state once** within its case/reference/episode, even when that state serves several strata; also report stratum-specific contrasts so a headroom result on T-only states is not attributed to J. Raw physical seed IDs shared across cases and references are the **outer paired clusters**; ticks, witness pairs and tapes are not independent episodes. Use 5,000 fixed-seed outer bootstrap resamples (`84903000`). Inside each resample, jointly resample 16 Bank-B tape indices for all actions at a state and all strata sharing it. Report outer-only and two-level 95% intervals, per-state paired-tape standard errors, Bank-A 4/4 and Bank-B 8/8 split results, and Bank-P two-tape half-selector results. The two-level interval covers state sampling and Bank-B Monte Carlo noise **conditional on A/P selection**; it does not cover A/P selector-seed variation or model misspecification. Never bootstrap branch rows as independent episodes.

A positive extreme-pair economic gate also requires the two Bank-A four-tape halves to agree on the direction of the full-A-selected pair in ≥75% of rows with prevalidation `|gap_A|≥6`, separately in every case/reference/stratum cell; fewer than 12 such rows makes that cell `INSUFFICIENT`. Report half-bank pair-identity changes and the Bank-B 8/8 sign stability without treating them as independent confirmation. For I, both Bank-P two-tape half-selected roots must have nonnegative pooled Bank-B advantage over F1 and no case/reference cell mean worse than −6. These diagnostics limit finite-forecast sensitivity; they cannot prove all possible selector seeds would agree. A positive pooled economic claim requires a two-level 95% lower endpoint >0. Wide intervals or unstable selection are inconclusive, never a zero-effect finding.

Practical floors are *simulation-unit design choices*, not calibrated money: **6 units** equals one unavailable-machine interval; **5% of H ticks** is 2.4 timing/allocation opportunities per episode; **12/24 episodes** avoids a claim from a few congested trajectories. For the stronger J claim, prelock a lower prevalence floor of **1% of H ticks plus 12/24 episodes** per case/reference because simultaneous binding PM competition is more specific; this narrower floor must be disclosed with the claim. These gates are not tuned to V22. If the researcher rejects them, use separately declared seeds for a descriptive estimation phase only, then prelock independent confirmation; never change a gate after seeing its outcome.

1. **Integrity:** independent replay verifies source/protocol/config hashes, all admissions/actions, complete catalogue, exact matching-based B/J witnesses, role-hash selection, all bank tapes/actions, forced branches, transitions, costs, H/tail treatment and terminal status. Audit failure is `INVALID`; timeout/cap is `INCOMPLETE`. Neither yields a scientific conclusion.
2. **S reachability/consequence:** in **every** case/reference cell, T and A each require ≥12/24 episodes and episode-averaged ≥5% H ticks. Their separately selected Bank-B extreme gaps each require cell mean ≥6, pooled two-level lower endpoint >0, no adverse cell mean and the Bank-A stability rule. B and J are reported regardless. A **binding** claim additionally requires B's same exposure and economic floors. A **same-tick PM timing–assignment** claim requires J ≥12/24 episodes and ≥1% H ticks, its own Bank-B mean ≥6 per cell, pooled lower endpoint >0 and the stability rule. Failure of a stronger scope never changes an already qualified narrower T/A result. If an extreme pair is unstable, report sentinel and catalogue-size diagnostics and label the economic gate `UNRESOLVED`, not “all legal choices have equal value.”
3. **I selection:** Bank-P root must improve Bank-B cost over F1 by ≥6 in every case/reference cell, pooled two-level lower endpoint >0, and meet the half-selector rule. For an I claim about binding or joint choices, the same root advantage must also pass on the B or J selected-state subset, respectively. Its Bank-B direction on the frozen Bank-A pairs must agree for ≥75% of rows with prevalidation `|gap_A|≥6`, separately for each *claimed* T/A/B/J stratum and cell; <12 rows is `INSUFFICIENT`. A Bank-B zero or sign-unstable pair counts as uncertain and is reported, not silently excluded. Failure qualifies only this selector/budget as unproven, with S retained.
4. **F versus F2:** report `D_B(F2,best_A)` and `D_B(F2,best_P)` separately by cell and T/A/B/J subset, with two-level intervals, 6-unit practical floor and forecast compute. A qualified **forecast headroom** claim requires the A contrast ≥6 in every cell and pooled lower endpoint >0; a qualified **four-tape selector advantage** requires the same for P. A B- or J-scoped F claim must meet those conditions on that selected-state subset as well; an overall F pass cannot be transferred to a rare binding subset. Neither F contrast is a veto on S or I. A matched-compute physical pilot is required for deployment or algorithm-superiority claims.

### Decision tree and permissible conclusions

Apply the rows in order to **claims**, while still reporting every estimand, even when a gate fails. `PASS` below means the full reachability, economic, uncertainty and stability conditions above; `UNRESOLVED` includes inadequate precision or catalogue-selection instability.

| Branch | Permissible conclusion and next step |
| --- | --- |
| Audit `INVALID` or `INCOMPLETE` | No scientific result. Preserve partials and failure reason. Correct only by a documented protocol/source amendment and a wholly fresh, independently seeded run; do not reinterpret partial outcomes. |
| T and A both fail or are unresolved | Broad post-admission timing–allocation quality is unqualified **in these cases/reference populations and this continuation**. Report each exposure/economic component; stop this qualification. No inference of universal setting failure. |
| T passes; A fails or is unresolved | PM start-versus-WAIT value is supported at its stated scope. Capacity-allocation value and the joint claim are unqualified; do not open a joint learner pilot from T alone. |
| A passes; T fails or is unresolved | Contested-worker allocation value is supported at its stated scope. PM timing and the joint claim are unqualified; do not call A a pure skill effect. |
| T and A pass; B fails or is unresolved | Two decision types have value, but a genuinely **binding** worker tradeoff has not been established. Claim separate timing and contested allocation only; no binding demand–capacity claim. |
| T, A and B pass; J fails or is unresolved | Binding allocation is supported. A direct **same-tick PM timing–assignment** interaction remains unqualified. Multi-period interaction is possible but untested here; no claim of it from separate T/A/B witnesses. |
| S passes at its stated scope; I fails, is unstable or insufficient | The setting has reached, economically material choices at that scope; this four-tape present-state rule has not reliably selected them. Preserve S. A new prelock may test more precise forecasting, information or representation, but do not launch a physical gain claim or declare the setting poor. |
| S and I pass; both F contrasts fail or are unresolved | Present-information selection beats F1 at the declared budget, while advantage over the fixed F2 root is unproven. Retain S and I. Any future claim may instead target compute/sample efficiency or another specified objective; F2 superiority needs new matched-compute evidence. |
| S and I pass; A forecast contrast passes but P contrast fails | An eight-tape higher-compute opportunity beyond F2 is validated conditionally; the four-tape selector has not realized that advantage. This does not qualify deployable or learned improvement over F2. |
| S and I pass; P contrast passes but A contrast fails | The independently validated four-tape selector contrast is supported; the eight-tape Bank-A contrast did not qualify. Report the finite-bank inconsistency and compute costs; require a new prelock before any broader forecast-headroom claim. |
| S and I pass; both F contrasts pass | Conditional forecast headroom and this present-state selector advantage over F2 are supported at the declared budgets and scope. Propose one separately prelocked paired **physical episode** pilot with matched information/action/compute and full-information, F1/F2 and architecture controls. No learner, TEST or campaign starts automatically. |

The first six rows establish the **scope of S** before I or F is interpreted. J prevalence or economic failure cannot erase valid T/A/B evidence; conversely T and A passing at separate states cannot be promoted to a same-tick interaction. An I/F result on nonbinding states cannot strengthen a binding/J claim. No result here estimates learnable policy gain.

A failure of Bank-P versus F2 appears only in F; it is never a premise for rejecting S. Passing S or I does not imply learnable policy gain. A full physical pilot must separately estimate **contract effect** (if FT/1 is activated), **solution effect** within one contract, and **representation/feedback attribution**. Same-action/full-information direct control, threshold/risk, strong scheduling and rollout controls are required; always-request has a different admission history/action set and must be labelled accordingly. Prelock pilot training/selection/DEV/held-out/TEST roles, practical H+tail gates, checkpoint rule, independent training roots, sample/parameter/forecast/inference/wall-time budgets, independent replay and stopping before outcomes. The 6-unit conditional branch floor must not be copied into an episode-gain gate.
