# Prelocked v6 joint demand–capacity learner pilot

Status: locked before v6 teacher labels, DEV or held-out outcomes, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). Proposal: `joint_demand_capacity_proposal_v6.md`. V5's gate passed but provides only finite-forecast representation headroom. V1 TEST and v2–v4 gates remain closed. No new TEST is authorized.

## Hypotheses, contract and arms

H1: explicit interaction between two simultaneous machine–technician service starts improves finite-budget H+tail decisions relative to an edge-additive actor under equal teacher information and action set, especially reciprocal. H2: it improves over a flat full-information actor given the same labels/budget. H3: the interaction term fits held-out F2 finite-forecast pair interactions better than the additive ablation. Failure of any claimed mechanism prevents an attribution claim.

Use unchanged `shared_maintenance_requests_v1` and `pending_matching_completion_v1` physics, fixed threshold PM submissions, automatic CM, complete legal N4/K2 matching/WAIT mask, H=48, 16-tick inherited tail and unmodified economic cost. No early-PM expansion, fees, hidden state, forced shutdown or future realized event input. Main arms: J6 shared machine/worker encoders, edge scores plus explicit two-edge interaction; A6 shared encoders and wider *additive only* edge scorer with near-matched active parameter count; G6 full-information flat direct actor of near-matched parameter count. All use identical raw present actor inputs, legal mask and F2 root-score training rows. J6/A6 WAIT score is zero; G6 subtracts its WAIT output from all action scores. Student deployment is deterministic masked argmax with zero forecasts. F2, risk matching F1, frozen V14 A, and the already-selected v1 diagnostic G (threshold + learned coordinator) and v1 D (always-request + learned coordinator) are same-panel references. Replay all three existing training-root checkpoints for G/D with their original request contracts and verify checkpoint hashes; do not retrain or select them on this panel. G/D used different training resources and D a different request set, so they are contextual rather than equal-budget attribution arms. F2 need not be beaten for a finite-budget learning claim. No arm is a two-policy MARL system.

Architecture fixed before outcomes: J6 and A6 share machine MLP 21→32→16 with tanh and worker MLP 4→16→16 with tanh. Each legal edge input concatenates machine/worker embeddings with known service duration and restoration (34 values). J6 edge MLP 34→32→1 with tanh and pair MLP 64→32→1 with tanh on the two 32-dimensional edge hidden vectors; its complete-matching score is the sum of selected edge outputs plus pair output when two starts. A6 edge MLP 34→90→1 with tanh and scores by edge sum only. G6 flattens the same 4×21 machine plus 2×4 worker values and uses MLP 92→33→33→21 with tanh. Score each catalogue action in its fixed canonical order; all masked actions are excluded. Report exact active parameter counts; no unused parameters to fake matching. The pair term's worker identities are encoded in the edge vectors, not an added feature.

## Teacher, target and fixed learning budget

For each new training root/case, generate 16 full F2 H+tail episodes. At every H state retain current machine/worker inputs, full legal mask and all F2 legal root scores from 16 common six-tick scenarios. If only WAIT is legal, its target is zero. For each legal action a, target y(a) = [Q_F2(WAIT) − Q_F2(a)] / 20; ineligible targets are ignored. This is a same-bank *relative finite-forecast score*, not calibrated economic value. Train each arm against **all legal scores** by mean squared error averaged across legal actions within each state, then across states in a batch. No action weighting, teacher-action cross-entropy, target clipping, DAgger/relabeling, custom auxiliary loss, reward shaping or hyperparameter search. Exact student score construction for WAIT is zero; one-action states contribute zero loss/gradient. Use Adam 3e-4, batch 128, exactly 64 epochs and identical shuffled dataset order per case/root across arms. Save epochs 16/32/64. Select the checkpoint with lowest deterministic H+tail mean on all 16 DEV physical seeds in its case; ties favor earlier epoch. DEV is for checkpoint selection only. Teacher source/model forecasts and all root scores must be audited. Report data examples, legal target count, gradient updates, training loss, CPU time, teacher forecast transitions and physical transitions; equal examples/updates is not equal wall or total compute.

## Evaluation, uncertainty and predeclared gates

Primary estimands: paired equal-case H+tail economic cost J6−A6 and J6−G6, averaged equally over three training roots and 32 raw physical seeds per case. Report reciprocal and specialist/generalist separately, plus each root's paired mean. F2/V14/F1/G/D use the same physical panel; compare costs and selection time descriptively, marking G/D as historical fixed checkpoints with their own budgets/contracts. Secondary: H availability/failures, PM/CM waiting, backlog, service starts, legal-service WAIT, training curves, selection/training/teacher CPU time, model size and forecast transitions. Teacher-visited held-out states provide a *separate diagnostic*: absolute error in the finite-forecast two-edge interaction Q(pair)−Q(e)−Q(f)+Q(WAIT), for J6 and A6 under the same F2 score bank, averaged across all legal pair roots. These teacher-state errors do not measure on-policy score calibration or explain H+tail causally.

For physical uncertainty, resample the **raw physical seed ID as one cluster across cases, all arms and all three trained roots**, 5,000 draws with seed 73218000. Report percentile 95% intervals conditional on the trained models and the three root means separately; do not treat correlated arms/cases/roots as independent episodes. For interaction-score error, cluster by F2 physical episode and report case-specific means; it is mechanistic support, not a replacement for H+tail.

The gate to independent training replication requires **all**:
1. J6−A6 overall ≤−10 H+tail, reciprocal ≤−20, specialist/generalist ≤+5; at least two of three training-root overall means <0; physical-cluster 95% upper endpoint <0.
2. J6−G6 overall ≤−10, reciprocal ≤−20, specialist/generalist ≤+5; at least two of three root overall means <0; physical-cluster 95% upper endpoint <0.
3. Reciprocal held-out F2-state mean absolute pair-interaction error for J6 ≤80% of A6, with the ratio defined from pooled legal pair roots across all three trained roots. If A6 error is zero, this condition fails.

The ±10/20 cost thresholds are practical targets below v5's +0.57 per-all-state six-tick forecast difference times 48 H decisions; that product is a heuristic scale, **not** a predicted H+tail gain or bound. No gate requires beating F2, but its same-panel gap and compute must be disclosed. A failed gate ends v6 without independent training replication/TEST. A passed gate requires full fresh training replication and the same cost/mechanism conditions on the replication panel before any TEST proposal. Do not lower gates, select favorable cases/roots, alter labels/loss/model/checkpoint rule or tune on held-out/TEST outcomes.
