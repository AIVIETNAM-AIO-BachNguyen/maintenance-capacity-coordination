# V4 forecast-informed demand/commitment pilot — locked protocol

Status: LOCKED BEFORE ANY V4 TEACHER LABEL, DEV OR HELD-OUT OUTCOME, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). Companion formulation: `forecast_demand_commitment_proposal_v4.md`. V1–v3 outcomes motivate this distinct teacher-amortization question and the predeclared practical scale. Their prior panels cannot select v4 checkpoints, alter this protocol after outcomes or revise any earlier gate. V1 TEST remains closed. The operating contract is unchanged: two N4/K2 cases (`reciprocal_scale2`, `specialist_generalist_scale2`), fixed-threshold persistent PM requests, automatic CM, complete legal partial matching/WAIT, non-preemptive skilled service, economic cost, H=48 and the same 16-tick threshold/risk tail. No future realized failure tape is supplied to an actor, critic or teacher; F2 samples independent forecast events.

## Questions, arms and estimands

H1: Can a learned actor amortize six-tick F2's same-contract decision rule into much cheaper deployment without a large H+tail cost loss? H2: Does shared machine-demand/worker-commitment factorization improve this finite-label learning versus a flat full-information actor? H3: Do numerical remaining-commitment inputs contribute beyond free/busy flags and the feasibility mask?

- S4: the unchanged v3 `StructuredActor` with all present machine and worker inputs.
- R4: the same actor/parameter count, with only actor machine fields 7 and 20 and worker field 1 zeroed as in v3; critic is unused. Free/busy indicators, legal mask, other current facts and training labels are unchanged.
- G4: the unchanged v3 `DirectActor` with the same raw actor inputs and legal mask, 1.8% more actor parameters than S4/R4.
- F2: fixed-threshold plus 16-scenario six-tick rollout teacher and same-panel control. When multiple roots are legal, it evaluates each and then follows threshold/risk matching; when only one is legal, it chooses it without a forecast. It is a finite known-model reference, not an optimum.
- A frozen V14 count-calibrated and F1 risk matching: same-panel external cost controls; different training/planning histories are disclosed.

Within each case/training seed, S4/R4/G4 receive **identical** F2-generated state/label rows. Only the actor is trained; no PPO or critic update. The loss is ordinary categorical negative log-likelihood of the deterministic F2 selected matching under the actor's exact masked softmax. One-action states have zero gradient but remain in the dataset and counts. No class weights, reward shaping, soft-target temperature, augmentation, replay aggregation, teacher threshold changes or hyperparameter search. The teacher's root forecast scores are retained for audit and descriptive regret, not used as extra labels or loss. Student deployment is deterministic masked argmax with zero forecasts.

Primary estimands on one fresh physical panel: paired equal-case H+tail economic cost S4−G4, S4−R4 and S4−F2, averaged equally over the three training seeds for learned arms and raw physical seeds. Lower is better. F2 has no training seed and is repeated as the same paired control in each model comparison. Secondary: cost by case and training seed, H availability/failures, PM/CM wait, H/end backlog, starts, legal-service WAIT, teacher agreement on F2-visited evaluation states, training-label NLL, actor parameter count, teacher/evaluation forecast transitions, data generation/training/inference wall time and H/tail physical transitions. Forecast scores are finite-scenario estimates, never treated as true Q values or calibrated machine economic values.

## Uncertainty and predeclared gates

Report the three training-root means separately. For conditional physical uncertainty, resample the **raw physical seed ID as one cluster across cases, arms and training roots**, 5,000 paired bootstrap draws with analysis seed 73198000. This interval conditions on the trained models; it is not an uncertainty interval over new training roots. Report both.

The gate to independent training replication passes only when **all** conditions hold on the audited held-out pilot panel:

1. S4−G4 mean H+tail cost ≤ −30.0; both case means <0; at least two of three training-root means <0; paired physical-cluster 95% interval upper endpoint <0.
2. S4−R4 mean H+tail cost ≤ −10.0; both case means <0; at least two of three training-root means <0; paired physical-cluster interval upper endpoint <0. This is specifically numerical remaining-time evidence, not all capacity context.
3. S4−F2 mean H+tail cost ≤ +20.0, each case ≤ +30.0, and measured S4 action-selection CPU time per H+tail episode ≤20% of F2's action-selection time on the same physical panel. Report teacher forecast generation cost separately; a low deployment cost does not imply low total research compute.

If the pilot gate fails, no independent training replication or v4 TEST opens and no threshold is revised. If it passes, repeat the entire data-generation, training, DEV-selection and evaluation procedure on the reserved independent roots/seeds, then require the same three conditions on the replication panel before proposing a TEST. A later TEST still needs explicit authorization for this protocol. Do not use DEV, held-out or TEST to alter architecture, labels, seed selection or budgets.
