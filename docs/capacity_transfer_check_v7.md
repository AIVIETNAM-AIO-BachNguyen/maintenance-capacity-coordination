## Locked go/no-go

The gate to fresh training replication requires all of the following on the audited pilot:

1. Target S−G3 overall ≤−30 H+tail, each target-case mean <0, at least two of three source-training-root overall means <0, and its raw-physical-cluster percentile 95% interval upper endpoint <0. The −30 practical threshold is the original v3 threshold, not lowered after v3's result.
2. Transfer contrast overall ≤−10 H+tail, each source→target pair ≤0, and its raw-physical-cluster 95% upper endpoint <0. This asks whether the structured gap improves by a material amount under both preselected duration shifts; cost levels across different configurations are not directly pooled without the paired within-configuration differences.
3. Target S−R overall ≤−10 H+tail, both target-case means ≤0, at least two of three root overall means <0, and its raw-physical-cluster 95% upper endpoint <0. This is required before a *numerical commitment-time* benefit can be claimed, not a claim about all capacity context.

Failure of any condition ends v7 without new training replication or TEST. No gate is lowered, no target configuration or root is dropped, no checkpoint is changed, and no v1 TEST or v3/v6 held-out seed is used for tuning. If the pilot passes, retrain S/R/G3 from scratch on the two original source configurations using the unchanged v3 PPO architecture and budget: three fresh roots 73310000–02; each source/arm/root exactly 8,192 H training decisions, PPO n_steps 256, minibatch 64, four epochs/update, Adam 3e-4, gamma .99, GAE .95, clip .2, entropy .01, same critic and seed derivation `role_seed(root, "structured_train_v3:<source_case>:<episode>")`. Select checkpoint at 2,048/4,096/8,192 using 16 fresh DEV raw seeds 73311000–15 by lowest source-case H+tail mean, ties to earlier milestone. Independently audit training, then evaluate all source/target cases on 32 fresh raw seeds 73312000–31 and apply the **same three gate conditions**. A passed replication only makes a separately authorized TEST proposal eligible; it does not open TEST.
