# Focused starting references

These are starting points, not a systematic review or established novelty claim.

1. Ruiz Rodríguez et al., *Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning based Predictive Maintenance on parallel machines*, Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing 78, 102406 (2022). [Author repository](https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/54345), DOI `10.1016/j.rcim.2022.102406`. Relevant to machine-level maintenance decisions and technician allocation using PPO. Do not attribute QMIX or the inherited request lifecycle to this paper without checking the original methods.
2. Wang and Gombolay, *Stochastic Resource Optimization over Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks for Failure-Predictive Maintenance Scheduling*, ICAPS 2022. [Publisher](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839). Relevant to AirME, graph-based scheduling, shared crews, WAIT/skip decisions and PPO-derived training. Shared maintenance, graph policies and retaining resources for future demand are already prior work.

This project seeks an evaluated coordination framework. Clearly distinguish the proposed operational contract, information exchange, architecture and empirical findings from standard RL methods. A framework name or adding two policy modules does not establish a contribution by itself.
