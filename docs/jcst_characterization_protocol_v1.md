## Questions and hypotheses

Estimate how operating lifetime relative to service duration, technician scarcity/skills, component heterogeneity, economics, initial ages, horizon and scale change production, failure, servicing and complete-action opportunities. Estimate whether timing, component, technician and joint/intertemporal choices occur and have stable conditional cost consequences. Estimate how much present-information forecast selection and additional compute help, without assuming they do. Hypotheses are directional descriptions, not mandatory positive gates: (H1) scarcity/restricted skills reduce simultaneous feasible starts and increase failed waiting relative to capacity surplus; (H2) homogeneous components/workers narrow C/W assignment effects; (H3) high PM cost reduces preventive service under competitive controls; (H4) longer forecast budget can change choices but may have diminishing or adverse independent cost returns. Negative controls and null/imprecise findings are retained.

## Controllers and information

All see the same full current state, legal matching set and public config. WAIT and corrective-only are sanity/restricted behavior. Periodic calendar PM periods 4 and 8, with staggered phase (m+c), and age-threshold PM ages 2 and 5 are behavior baselines; all prioritize failed starts with score 10, then maximize admitted starts, minimize total service duration, then lexicographic action. Risk/duration matching is the fixed public additive heuristic with exact full-root enumeration and lexicographic tie. Bounded rollout enumerates every root, uses role-separated forecast seeds, fixed risk continuation, and tie by total forecast cost then lexicographic action. Rollout budgets are R2=(2 scenarios,2 transitions of depth), R4=(4,4), R8=(8,6); the depth number includes the forced root. These are exact root enumeration, not stochastic-horizon optimum. Per-decision cap is 20,000 simulated transitions; exceeding it is INCOMPLETE, never silently truncation. Same-information comparisons are reported separately from same-model-access comparisons (risk versus rollout has different model use), matched-compute comparisons (same forecast cap), and unmatched budget frontiers.

A selection bank of 64 physical seeds per case chooses one periodic and one threshold candidate by lowest mean H+tail cost, with lexicographic smaller parameter tie. Both candidates get equal physical decisions and identical seeds. The choice is locked before confirmation. The same bank is never used to estimate confirmed performance. Fixed heuristics are not tuned. No learner, oracle input or future physical uniform reaches a controller. A nondeployable hindsight diagnostic, if reported, is clearly labeled and excluded from policy gains.

## Motifs and economic estimands

T pairs differ only by a healthy PM start versus its removal. C changes component on the same machine and technician, with PM/CM status disclosed. W changes technician for the same target. J is a same-tick capacity conflict in which two distinct machines cannot both start under any reassignment. A holds the same started-machine set and start count fixed, comparing complete technician/component assignments whose per-machine service-duration maps differ. R compares different release boundaries. P compares START/WAIT actions whose next-boundary feasible capacity differs. Q exists only when a faster compatible worker is currently busy while a slower compatible worker is free; its WAIT arm is a *strategy*: hold that target until the faster worker releases, then start it if legal, while the disclosed risk controller handles other available starts. Q cost is never described as the value of naked WAIT. For joint/intertemporal nonadditivity, select two disjoint feasible starts a,b; estimate C(a+b)-C(a)-C(b)+C(WAIT) under common tapes and the same risk continuation. The contrast has additive immediate root cost for distinct machines; later nonadditivity is conditional on the four commitment paths, not a physics-disabled counterfactual. Report changes in next-boundary feasible actions and release times, and avoid calling this a universally isolated capacity effect.

For each motif and case/reference: structural prevalence is episode-averaged H-tick fraction and fraction of episodes with any witness; conditional consequence is Bank-B/C paired tape mean cost of A-selected pair, with A orientation held fixed; sentinel is cost-independent paired difference. Selection ability is the P-root versus risk-root paired cost on B/C and directional accuracy for A-meaningful pairs. A meaningful contrast means absolute Bank-A gap at least one unavailable-machine interval c_U, a simulation-unit scale anchor, not a calibrated monetary benefit. Present distributions and uncertainty rather than replacing absent motifs with zeros. Do not multiply local gaps by occurrence frequency to claim episode gain.

## Measurement and statistics

For every episode/controller/case record working/breakdown/service machine intervals, component failures, PM/CM starts and completions, unfinished jobs, technician occupied/idle intervals, duration and release histograms, failed waiting and recovery delay, simultaneous failed components, catalogue size, T/C/W/J/A/R/Q/P opportunity counts, H cost, tail cost and each cost component, endpoint ages/failed jobs, root evaluations, scenarios, simulated transitions, selection time and episode wall time. Distinguish observed demand under a policy from exogenous operating hazard. All source states, actions and paths are serialized; no crafted state contributes prevalence.

Physical seed/episode is the outer cluster. Report per-case estimates before equal-case pooled summaries; cases are design strata and equally weighted only when pooling is meaningful. Use 2,000 fixed-role paired physical-seed bootstrap resamples for 95% intervals; for conditional gaps, also report forecast tape SE and a nested outer-cluster/within-state-tape bootstrap interval (1,000 resamples). Do not treat ticks, branches or tapes as physical replicates. For the four primary motif families T/C/W/J, use simultaneous max-|t| bootstrap intervals over the four per-case contrasts (or Holm-adjusted p-values for explicitly directional confirmation); A/R/Q/P and frontier are descriptive with 95% intervals. Report effect magnitudes and the c_U scale anchor, not sign alone. Uncovered uncertainty includes hazard-model misspecification, cost calibration, initial-population choice and field external validity.

No positive benchmark gate is imposed on Stage 1/2. Confirmation criteria are locked now for any *confirmed conditional economic* claim: motif present in at least 12 of 128 confirmation episodes in the case/reference stratum; Bank-C oriented mean gap at least c_U; simultaneous adjusted 95% lower bound above zero; no adverse reference mean if pooling references. These floors represent one unavailable-machine interval and a minimal spread over physical episodes, not calibrated practical value. Otherwise report absent, imprecise or sub-threshold. A physical strong-control advantage requires paired H+tail mean cost reduction at least c_U, adjusted 95% interval excluding zero, in each claimed case; no result here qualifies a learned framework. An efficiency claim needs a future external latency/sample tolerance, so this campaign only reports the frontier and cannot assert deployment noninferiority. Case recommendation is based on documented regime diversity, motif reachability and stable conditional consequence, plus tractable strong controls and negative controls; rollout losing is not itself a reason to choose a case. Before outcomes, the candidate roles are fixed: base/generalist/restricted are core candidates; shortlife/scarce/scale4/pm_expensive/downtime_high/staggered are stress candidates; memoryless/homogeneous/surplus/longlife are negative or boundary controls. All 13 remain reported. A core candidate becomes recommended only if integrity passes and at least timing, component and technician choices are structurally reachable on at least one reference population, with at least one confirmed conditional economic contrast and a usable strong-control compute/cost report. If these conditions fail, label it unqualified rather than replace it with the most favorable case. Stress and negative cases are retained for falsification even if they show no gain. An INVALID or INCOMPLETE case is excluded from confirmed claims pending new collection, never silently dropped from the atlas.
