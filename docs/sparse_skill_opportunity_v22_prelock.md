# V22 sparse-skill structural opportunity qualification: prelock

Status: **locked before v22 implementation, smoke or simulation outcomes**. V1–v21 gates remain final; v21 is closed no-go. This is a bounded simulator-only qualification for one possible next same-tick learning pilot, not evidence that a learned policy helps. No new TEST is authorized.

## Question and reason for this contract

The audited v20 training bank has 508 states. A post-v21 training-only enumeration found 1 state where two feasible first physical assignments leave the same number (at least two) of immediately feasible remaining edges but different maximum additional matching cardinalities, after removing the chosen machine and technician and ignoring decoder order. Thus the existing bank supplies almost no *within-state* contrast for the user's concern that edge mean/max/count may hide whether machines share a scarce technician. This does not mean other economically important feedback choices are absent, or that the v16 incidence-aware graph cannot encode the relation. V16's engineering motif test distinguishes constructed capacity-one/two graphs, while the v20 information-path probe showed trained embeddings respond to loss of a sole worker; neither proves useful economic use.

V22 first asks whether the **unchanged simulator law and threshold admission** can generate enough reachable, within-state structural contrasts under a fixed, more heterogeneous skill matrix to justify training a new policy. It changes compatibility, an operating configuration, not failure, restoration, cost, timing or action semantics. No fees, forced shutdowns, hidden information or future-event input are introduced. If the qualification passes, a separate full learning protocol must be prelocked before any label collection, PPO or DEV/held-out evaluation. No v22 learning run is authorized by this qualification alone.

## Simulator qualification and fixed decision

Run the threshold-request + F1 risk-matching present-state controller for exactly 32 fresh raw physical seeds 83801000..83801031 in **each** case. Preserve the H48 physical decisions and inherited tail. Before every H decision, form the threshold-admitted state and its immediate compatibility/idle/queue mask. For every feasible first pair, remove its machine and technician and count the remaining feasible pairs **without** canonical index restriction. Compute exact maximum cardinality of that residual bipartite graph (at most two technicians remain). A qualifying tick contains two distinct first pairs whose residual edge counts are equal and at least two, but whose residual maximum matching cardinalities differ. Count qualifying ticks, distinct episodes with at least one such tick and the residual edge-count strata. Separately log the canonical higher-index residual counts for decoder relevance, without using them to change the gate. The gate passes only if **both** cases have at least 16 qualifying ticks and at least 12 of 32 episodes with a qualifying tick. These are structural exposure requirements, not economic-effect estimates. Do not read or select on H+tail costs in this stage.
