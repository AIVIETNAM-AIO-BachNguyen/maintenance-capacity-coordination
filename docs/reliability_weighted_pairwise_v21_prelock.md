# V21 reliability-weighted pairwise matching supervision: prelock

Status: **locked before any v21 implementation, smoke, training, DEV or held-out outcomes**. V1–v20 verdicts and gates stay closed. This is the one current scientific experiment. No new TEST is authorized.

## Question and single intervention

V20's trained C20 encoder had an effective information path for busy-technician time and provisional loss of a machine's sole feasible worker (`docs/v20_information_path_diagnostic.md`), yet its selected checkpoint fit the new training bank only slightly better than uniform and its physical cost was worse than C19/D20 (`docs/on_policy_matching_value_v20_findings.md`). On independent halves of the training tapes, pairwise cost signs were more reproducible when the first-half mean gap was large (`docs/v20_training_label_fit_diagnostic.md`). These are descriptive observations, not evidence that a different loss will improve economic cost.

V21 changes **only the supervised matching-label loss** used for pretraining and the fixed 0.5 auxiliary term during PPO. C21/D21 receive a reliability-weighted pairwise loss built from the same audited v20 16-tape, eight-candidate training bank. L21 uses v20's listwise loss on that identical bank, actor, initialization, training physical tapes and PPO budget. C21 and D21 have identical graph modules and inputs; C21 refreshes machine/technician/candidate context after each provisional pair, D21 freezes root context while updating legality. CF21 loads C21 selected weights and freezes context only at inference. No new encoder, requester, reward, simulator contract, matching or future input is added. The hypothesis is that suppressing uncertain candidate comparisons improves the learner's economic matching decisions enough for feedback to matter. A gain on in-sample label fit alone does not establish this.

## Fixed pairwise objective

For each training row with n legal candidate matchings, use its 16 common future-event tapes. For candidate pair i<j, let d_t = cost_i,t − cost_j,t, Δ = mean_t d_t, and SE = sample_std(d_t)/sqrt(16). The target probability that i is better is q_ij = sigmoid(−Δ/20). Let s_i and s_j be the actor's exact canonical-sequence log probabilities for those complete matchings. Its pairwise prediction is p_ij = sigmoid(s_i−s_j). The fixed reliability weight is w_ij = |Δ|/(|Δ|+2SE+1e−8). If every weight in a row is zero, use equal weights for that row. The row loss is the w-normalized binary cross-entropy across its pairs, multiplied by log(n)/log(2), so equal candidate scores have loss log(n), matching the listwise uniform reference. Average rows in each minibatch. Detach targets, SE and weights. Retain all legal candidates including WAIT; no top-gap selection, hard sign filtering or future event enters the actor. This loss is a surrogate for cost ranking, not an unbiased estimate of expected episode cost. C21/D21 use it for 96 pretraining epochs and coefficient 0.5 during PPO; L21 uses v20 listwise loss at the same stages. Critic and physical reward never receive label targets.

## Contract, arms and controls

## Estimands, gates and replication

Primary outcome is paired H+tail economic cost, with negative C21 differences favorable. Evaluate frozen DEV-selected arms on 32 fresh held-out raw seeds per case/root, plus F1/F2. Use raw physical seed as the bootstrap cluster, preserving repeated case/root/arm pairing, and report three training-root means separately. Report absolute costs, H availability/failures, PM/CM queues and backlog, service starts, WAIT, matching cardinality, generalist allocation, DEV learning curves, training wall time/parameters/gradient and loss traces, inference latency and decoder/forecast compute. Same-weight token changes are descriptive; the physical C21−CF21 episode contrast tests economic value. No attention weight or in-sample fit substitutes for cost.

Independent training replication is eligible only if **all** audited fresh held-out conditions hold: C21−L21 ≤−10, both case means negative and physical-cluster 95% upper endpoint <0; C21−D21 ≤−20, both cases negative, at least two of three training-root means negative and upper <0; C21−CF21 ≤−10, both cases negative and upper <0; C21−C19/G18/B18/S18 pooled means negative; and C21 no more than +20 versus F2 overall. If any fail, close v21 without independent retraining or TEST. If all pass, independently retrain C21/D21/L21 on reserved new roots with identical fixed label bank and budgets, select on new replication DEV and evaluate on fresh replication physical seeds. Require C21−L21, C21−D21 and C21−CF21 pooled and both-case means negative with cluster upper <0 before separately requesting TEST authorization. Never open TEST automatically. Do not lower gates, choose a favorable case/root, tune on held-out or revise the training objective after outcomes.
