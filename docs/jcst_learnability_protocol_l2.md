# JCST/1.1 L2 learnability and WAIT diagnosis: pre-outcome protocol

Status: prelaunch lock candidate, 2026-10-26 01:54 UTC. This is a new campaign following closed L1. No L2 scientific outcomes have been collected. Source, configuration, protocol hashes and a final engineering timing check will be frozen in a prelaunch manifest before TRAIN/DEV. No sealed TEST, contract change, branch, commit, push or remote execution is permitted. The user's direct authorization permits bounded local Linux scientific execution after engineering PASS.

## Question and fixed problem

Primary question: with the unchanged L1 PPO recipe and S/F/A actors, does more training produce useful policies, and is frequent greedy WAIT due to forced availability, a high WAIT probability, or a near-tied action distribution? Cases are base and generalist separately. H=48, fixed risk tail B=16, all-healthy age-zero/free initial state, all complete legal matchings including WAIT and PM at every healthy age, full current/public information and no future-noise input are unchanged. The tail charge enters the last controlled reward once. Reward is negative cost divided by 100, gamma=1, lambda=.95 GAE, no terminal bootstrap. Physical evaluation is undiscounted H+tail total cost; GAE affects the variance/bias of advantage estimates, not the target physical cost. S, F and A use the exact L1 source actors and shared critic. F's complete-action encoding is an edge-indicator vector plus matching cardinality, not an action-ID one-hot vector as some L1 prose states; it uniquely identifies each matching and contains the same legal-action information. This documentation correction does not alter L1 outcomes.

## Fixed first round

Thirty independent-from-scratch trajectories: 2 cases × 3 arms × 5 training roots. Each reaches nested checkpoints 24,000, 96,000, 384,000 and 1,536,000 controlled decisions (500, 2,000, 8,000, 32,000 complete episodes). Checkpoints are not independent training replicates. PPO remains Adam 3e-4, four epochs, minibatch 128, clip .2, entropy coefficient .01, max global gradient norm .5, 25 complete episodes per update, reward divisor 100, gamma 1 and GAE lambda .95. No teacher, auxiliary objective, architecture change or reward change. One CPU worker, one Torch thread, no GPU. Training stochastic actions and PPO minibatch permutations retain the L1 algorithm; DEV evaluation occurs after all checkpoints are saved, so evaluation cannot change continuing training RNG. Dedicated Torch generators draw stochastic DEV/confirmation actions; physical failure randomness remains keyed by physical seed.

Every training episode stores a compact H/tail cost/component/action summary, and a deterministic, fixed sample of 10 full traces per root at episode indices 0–3, 499, 1999, 7999 and 31997–31999 for replay. All DEV/confirmation evaluation episodes retain full traces for independent replay. Optimization telemetry is measured at updates 1 and multiples of 20 (including all checkpoints): actor surrogate loss, critic loss, entropy, approximate KL, clip fraction, advantage/return scale, critic prediction error, actor/critic gradient norms before and after the unchanged global clip and relative parameter-update magnitude. All update rows retain total loss and advantage SD. This fixed subsampling bounds overhead; no outcome-dependent logging density.

At each checkpoint, evaluate every root on the same 16 fresh physical DEV seeds per case, both greedy normalized-logit argmax (lowest catalogue index on exact ties) and stochastic categorical sampling with four independent action seeds per physical seed. The four draws are nested under each physical seed. Record full H/tail/component costs, PM/CM/failure/downtime/backlog, action count, entropy, WAIT probability, chosen-action probability, top-two normalized-logit and probability gaps and legal catalogue size. Near-ties mean top-two normalized-logit gap ≤0.01; exact numerical ties mean ≤1e-6. Partition WAIT into forced (only WAIT legal) and discretionary, and label the latter by failed compatible free target, healthy state and all-workers-busy context. Controls threshold5, risk/duration, corrective-only and WAIT use the same DEV physical seeds once each. R2 is omitted in L2 because its forecast-heavy reference does not resolve PPO learnability; the closed L1 R2 result remains historical.

## Seeds and estimands

Fresh L2 physical base = 2^50. Physical seed = base + bank×10^12 + case_index×10^10 + training_root×10^7 + episode_index, with banks engineering 1, TRAIN 2, DEV 3, confirmation 4, intervention1 TRAIN 5, intervention1 DEV 6, intervention2 TRAIN 7, intervention2 DEV 8, replication TRAIN 9 and replication evaluation 10. Episode index <10^6; training root 0–4 for development and 5–9 for independent replication. Arm-specific Torch seeds derive from a separate base 2^49 plus bank, case, arm and root; stochastic action seeds derive from another base 2^48 plus bank, case, arm, root, checkpoint, physical-index and repetition. All values are below 2^64 and outside L1/C1 banks. Physical DEV seeds are paired across arms, roots and deployment modes; action draws never set physical noise.

Equal weight to the five training roots and 16 physical seeds for each DEV arm/case/checkpoint/mode; average the four action seeds within each physical seed. Selection separately within each case/arm minimizes this mean across eight checkpoint×deployment combinations, ties to fewer samples then greedy. A fixed 24k checkpoint evaluated in the same selected mode is the low-budget comparator. No best-root selection. Per-case results precede descriptive equal-case pooled results. Report stochastic-minus-greedy paired on physical seeds, retaining action-seed repetitions as nested rather than new physical replicates.

DEV eligibility for a single frozen bundle requires all integrity/audit checks and complete diagnostics plus **at least one** arm/case with selected checkpoint ≥96k, DEV mean improvement ≥20 cost units versus its 24k checkpoint in the same selected mode, ≥20 units versus WAIT, at least four of five roots each improving ≥10 versus its own 24k checkpoint on paired physical seeds, and mean controlled service starts >2 per episode in at least four roots. These DEV thresholds select a hypothesis to confirm; they are not confirmation evidence. The entire fixed S/F/A two-case selected bundle proceeds if eligible; no winner is chosen using confirmation. If none qualifies, close L2 without confirmation and identify the supported diagnostic class. No adaptive seed extension.

## Confirmation and replication

Before confirmation, freeze source, selected checkpoint/mode per arm/case, physical and action seed roles, estimands, plots and independent analysis code. Evaluate the frozen bundle on 128 fresh paired physical seeds per case, one greedy or four action seeds per physical seed according to selected mode. Also evaluate the 24k counterpart in the selected mode for each arm/root, and threshold5/risk/corrective/WAIT once per physical seed. Do not tune on confirmation. If any arm/case satisfies the useful-learning confirmation criterion below and audit PASS, independently retrain **all** S/F/A arms in both cases using five new roots (indices 5–9) to their frozen selected checkpoints, with the same recipe. Evaluate them on 128 further fresh physical seeds per case. This independent retraining is required before claiming recipe generalization; replaying the old checkpoints is only physical generalization. A failed confirmation closes the campaign without replication.

Primary confirmation family: 18 two-sided Bonferroni-adjusted intervals, 3 arms × 2 cases × (selected−24k, selected−WAIT) plus 2 cases × (S−F, S−A, S−threshold5). Cost differences favor the first item when negative. Practical useful learning requires selected−24k mean ≤−10, selected−WAIT mean ≤−20, and both adjusted upper bounds <0 in a case, with ≥4/5 roots individually improving against 24k. Threshold5 competitiveness is S−threshold5 mean ≤+10 and upper ≤+10; representation/mechanism require S−F/S−A mean ≤−10 and upper <0, respectively. Claims across both cases require each case to pass. All margins are uncalibrated simulator units, 10 being about 1.2–1.3% of C1 threshold cost; 20 is a modest stronger gain over WAIT and a DEV eligibility filter. These were fixed before L2 outcomes. Report partial results without transferring success between criteria or cases.

For independent-arm contrasts, use a two-way crossed bootstrap: jointly resample physical seed indices across arms/controls, independently resample five training roots within each arm. For same-arm selected−24k, resample the same root indices for both checkpoints. For stochastic mode, average its four action-seed repetitions within each physical seed before the bootstrap; report their within-seed variation separately. Controls are never replicated across roots. Use 4,000 bootstrap draws and fixed seed 2^47, percentile two-sided 1−.05/18 intervals. A separate audit recomputes raw means, bootstrap intervals and gate arithmetic. Replication uses the same estimands/gates on new training roots and physical seeds. No efficiency-success substitution is predeclared.

## Bounded interventions and stopping

At most two further TRAIN/DEV rounds are allowed only after the qualification's specific diagnosis and a timestamped pre-outcome amendment. Each is a single-factor change applied to all S/F/A arms with fresh TRAIN/DEV banks, five roots per arm/case and nested 24k/96k/192k checkpoints, with the same evaluation and selection rules. Candidate changes are learning rate/update strength, entropy coefficient, or a fixed deployment sampling rule; choose only one per round based on TRAIN/DEV evidence. The intervention must be technically interpretable and within the unchanged physical objective; no architecture or reward change. The campaign can stop earlier. Source bugs instead trigger preservation of affected artifacts and a uniform-source rerun, not a counted scientific intervention. No outcome-driven extension of any ladder or bank.

Total scientific execution cap is 72 elapsed hours across collection, evaluation, independent audit and any replication, excluding only user-requested pauses. One process/CPU worker, one Torch intra/inter-op thread, 8 GiB peak process RAM, 15 GiB total new artifact disk, and no GPU. Stop cleanly at limits, retaining partials as INCOMPLETE. Engineering timing from L1: 720,000 controlled decisions plus DEV took 1,479 s; a 64× first-round extrapolation is 26.3 h before richer logging, with ~1.8 GiB raw compressed artifacts from its 28 MiB L1 training directory. Two 192k interventions add at most about 6.6 h training; full independent replication adds up to another 26.3 h if the 1.536m bundle is selected. The remaining ~12 h is reserved for logging, evaluation, audit and contention. A final bounded Linux timing smoke must validate this estimate before launch; otherwise revise this protocol before outcomes or report the resource blocker.

Every run uses a fresh UTC timestamped directory, refuses overwrite and retains raw/partial records, checkpoints, manifest status COMPLETE/INCOMPLETE/INVALID, exact command, repo-relative paths, source/protocol/config/checkpoint hashes, Git status, packages, seed roles and resource counters. `tqdm` covers training and multiseed evaluation. Resume is prohibited unless hashes and duplicate-record checks are implemented; otherwise a failed panel restarts in a fresh directory. An independent replay auditor verifies sampled TRAIN traces and all evaluation traces against its own transition/cost calculation, action catalogue and policy checkpoint probabilities, seed separation, completeness and H/tail accounting. An independent statistics audit verifies selection and decisions. Self-checks are labeled as such. L1 and sealed TEST artifacts are read-only history.
