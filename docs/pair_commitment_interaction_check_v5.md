# Prelocked v5 bounded joint-service interaction check

Status: locked before v5 outcome, 2026-10-22 (Asia/Taipei). Proposal: `pair_commitment_interaction_proposal_v5.md`. This is one diagnostic, not a training pilot, independent replication, or TEST. Earlier outcomes and gates remain unchanged.

## Hypothesis, contract and comparison

The falsifiable hypothesis is that jointly starting two services creates forecast-relevant demand–capacity interaction not preserved by additive independent-edge scores, particularly in the reciprocal N4/K2 case. Use unchanged `shared_maintenance_requests_v1` and `pending_matching_completion_v1` physics, fixed threshold submissions, complete feasible matching/WAIT set, H=48 and the inherited 16-tick tail. No new physical actions, information restriction, price or reward. No future realized event tape reaches a controller.

At each F2-visited H state, call the inherited F2 rollout with its exact 16-scenario, six-tick selection bank and risk-matching continuation. If only WAIT is legal, record zero difference. If no legal two-start matching exists, additive and F2 decisions are identical because their scores for WAIT and every singleton are identical. Otherwise retain *all* legal root scores. For WAIT/singletons, additive score equals the F2 selection score. For a legal two-start matching (e,f), additive score is Q(e)+Q(f)−Q(WAIT). Break additive ties in F2 root enumeration order (risk-matching root first, then catalogue order). The F2 selected action remains the inherited `choose` result. Do not fit or calibrate the additive approximation.

For every two-start-eligible state, score the F2 and additive selected roots on the **same new independent 32-scenario six-tick bank**, using the unchanged transition, fixed threshold submissions on the first tick and future ticks, and risk matching after the root. The state-level difference is additive minus F2 independent-bank forecast cost; positive favors explicit complete-matching assessment. Count both roots and every forecast transition. This is a finite-model forecast comparison conditional on F2-visited states, not realized H+tail, an optimum gap or a trained-policy comparison.

## Estimand, uncertainty and gate

Primary stratum: reciprocal states with at least one legal two-start matching. Primary effect is the mean independent-bank additive-minus-F2 forecast cost over these states, weighting each state equally. Secondary: corresponding mean over **all** 48 reciprocal states per episode (zero when no two-start option), same quantities in specialist/generalist, interaction-score distribution, choice-disagreement rate, two-start frequency, H+tail F2 cost and runtime. The two-start stratum is predeclared from the mechanism, not selected on regret.

For uncertainty, resample the 16 **raw physical seed IDs** as paired clusters across cases, keeping all 48 states, both choices and overlapping forecast windows together. Use 5,000 draws with seed 73202000; for the conditional primary mean, recompute numerator/denominator after cluster resampling. Report the percentile 95% interval and 16 episode means/counts, not per-state independent intervals. The gate to justify designing a new learner requires **all**: reciprocal two-start frequency at least 10%; additive and F2 choices differ on at least 20% of those states; reciprocal conditional independent-bank mean at least +1.5 cost units; reciprocal all-state independent-bank mean at least +0.20; and the primary clustered interval lower endpoint above zero. The specialist/generalist outcome is descriptive; it cannot be used to change this gate. A failure ends v5 without learner/TEST. A pass permits only a separately prelocked same-action learner pilot, not any claim of H+tail improvement or TEST eligibility.

Training, checkpoint selection, DEV, independent training replication and sealed TEST do not apply to this diagnostic; any learner requires its own new seed registry and protocol. V1 TEST and v4 held-out data cannot be used for this decision.
