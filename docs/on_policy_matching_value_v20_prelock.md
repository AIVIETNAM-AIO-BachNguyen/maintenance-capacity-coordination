# V20 recent-policy-support matching-value labels: locked successor

Status: locked after the independently audited v19 no-go and its descriptive sensitivity check, **before any v20 implementation, smoke, labels, training, DEV or held-out outcomes**. V1–v19 verdicts and gates stay closed. This is the one current research experiment. All data are simulator-generated; no operational dataset is required. No new TEST is authorized.

## Question and bank intervention

V19 kept the old C16-visited, 16-tape matching-label loss from drifting during PPO, but its selected C19 cross-entropy of 2.050/2.054 was near a per-row uniform reference of 2.063/2.079 in cases a/b, and C19−C18 physical cost was +1.79. A post-pilot sensitivity replay found 261/9,216 same-weight matching changes and 785 multiple-edge second-token opportunities, yet C19−CF19 physical cost failed its gate. These observations do not show whether the weak benefit comes from input information, graph encoding, off-policy label-state distribution or label predictability.

V20 replaces **one training input: the fixed matching-value label bank**. Collect a new training-only bank from states visited by the fixed, audited v19 C19 and D19 selected actors, in balanced source-policy/root/time-window strata, and include their matching recommendations among the eight candidate matchings. Keep the eight-candidate budget, 16 independent future-event tapes, fixed S16 continuation, H48+tail16 value, temperature 20, actor, graph updates, canonical sequence policy, supervised optimizer, 0.5 rehearsal during PPO, PPO budget and physics. This tests whether matching-value supervision covering recent-policy states and actions improves physical decisions; a positive result would not by itself separate state coverage from candidate coverage. It is not an on-policy RL value estimate, a new reward or a new physical action. No v19 held-out state, seed, action or cost enters label construction, training or checkpoint selection.

The training-bank change is compared with reselected C19/D19 checkpoints on fresh v20 DEV and held-out panels. C20 versus C19 is the direct combined bank-intervention contrast, because actor, per-case/root initializations, physical training reset tapes and PPO budget match. C20 versus newly trained D20 tests dynamic graph feedback at the same new-bank intervention; CF20 loads each selected C20 state dict into the frozen-context actor to isolate same-weight within-tick feedback. C19/D19, G18/B18/S18/P18 and F1/F2 are declared contextual controls. C20 and D20 have identical parameters and input; only C20 refreshes contextual scores after a provisional edge. Every learned/control arm except declared always-request Q18 uses threshold PM admission and the same physical matching/WAIT action set. No requester policy is trained.

## Operating contract and label acquisition

Use the unchanged `n5k3_residual_a/b` configurations with config seeds 74740000/74740001, N5/K3 heterogeneous skills, failure/restoration/service/cost law, non-preemptive PM/CM, threshold PM admission, H48+tail16 and all legal complete matchings including WAIT. A token only updates the provisional matching; the simulator advances and charges cost once after the final matching. STOP may end at zero, one or multiple pairs. Keep the canonical increasing-edge sequence probability, conditional sequence entropy and greedy sequence decoding. Never treat sequence log probability as marginal matching probability.

New label raw physical seeds are 81801000..81801047; smoke seeds 81800011/81800012. At every case, original training root 80710000..80710002, and fixed source actor C19/D19, replay each raw seed for H48 with its own policy and threshold admission. For each half-window 0–23 and 24–47, select one state by a deterministic v20 state-selection role seed among states with at least one legal first edge that leaves two distinct legal second edges. Freeze the state and its admission submissions before executing its source actor matching. Form a candidate pool from these fixed source-policy/root/window strata; sort within each stratum by a deterministic v20 hash and interleave strata in a fixed order to select exactly 273 case-a and 235 case-b states, matching the v18 bank size. Stop before training if eligible supply is insufficient or any source policy/root/window stratum is empty. Record all candidate states, selection hashes, source checkpoints and provenance. The quota mechanism must not use continuation costs or future tapes.

At each selected state enumerate all physically legal complete matchings and verify one-to-one canonical sequence coverage. Select up to eight unique matchings by fixed priority: WAIT; the C19, D19 and same-weight CF19 recommendations at that state; one otherwise-unselected matching of each cardinality 1, 2, 3 when available; then deterministic uniformly permuted legal fill to eight. If there are fewer than eight legal matchings, retain all. Use role-separated deterministic candidate seeds. For every candidate run 16 independent v20 future-event tapes, common across candidates at that state, forcing the candidate once and then continuing with the same frozen S16 actor used in v17/v18. No future tape or target value enters the actor observation. Target probability is softmax of negative mean H+tail cost divided by 20. Store per-tape H and tail costs, branch action, canonical tokens and target.

The full label stage has a four-hour timeout; retain the partial run on timeout and do not silently drop states. Audit the new bank before training by replaying source state/admission, every candidate and forced branch, accounting H+tail exactly and checking seed separation/source/config/protocol hashes. The 8-vs-8 tape reliability gate must pass **in each case**: winner agreement ≥0.30 and pairwise cost-sign agreement ≥0.63 excluding exact ties, matching v18's qualification. If it fails, close v20 without PPO or held-out. This is a label reliability gate, not a learning or economic result.

Primary paired H+tail differences are C20−C19 (new versus old bank), C20−D20 (trained dynamic versus frozen scoring under the new bank) and C20−CF20 (same weights, feedback at inference).
