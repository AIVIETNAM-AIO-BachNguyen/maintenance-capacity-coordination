## Distinct uncertainty and intervention

The independently audited v18 16-tape training bank qualified for learning, but C18's in-sample listwise cross-entropy was only 2.044 after pretraining (uniform 2.071, target entropy 1.902) and 2.652 at the DEV-selected PPO checkpoint. A fixed checkpoint-path replay on the *training bank* showed loss rising by the first PPO milestone and continuing upward through 8,192 decisions for C18, D18 and L18 in both cases. DEV cost did not move monotonically with that loss. This establishes ranking drift on the original training states, not its physical cause or a reason to alter v18's gate.

V19 tests one training-path change: add a fixed-strength listwise rehearsal term on the existing audited 16-tape matching labels to each PPO minibatch. The actor, decoder, observation, matching action set, labels, physical simulator and PPO hyperparameters remain unchanged. C19/D19 start from the exact C18/D18 per-case/per-root pretraining checkpoints and use the same 8,192-decision training reset tapes as those v18 trials. The sole intended training intervention is the rehearsal term. Separately trained C19 versus D19 tests dynamic feedback at matched intervention; CF19 loads each selected C19 state dict into frozen-context D16 to isolate same-weight feedback. Reselected C18/D18 checkpoints on fresh v19 DEV and held-out physical tapes are no-rehearsal references. V19 success requires physical economic benefit, not merely lower training-label loss.

This is a standard PPO actor with an auxiliary simulator-label ranking loss, not a new RL algorithm, a separate requester policy or a new physical decision. The matching candidates and their fixed S16 continuation values are only training supervision; neither future tape nor target value enters the actor input or simulator transition. No reward shaping, fees, physical reservation, forced service or input concealment is added.
