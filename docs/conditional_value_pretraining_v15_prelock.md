## Distinct hypothesis and prior boundary

V15 asks whether direct **training-time economic supervision of conditional second-edge ranking**, followed by unchanged PPO on physical episode reward, lets the current machine-demand/technician-capacity actor use partial-matching context. This is not root-action imitation of F2's six-tick choice (v4) or F2 root matching scores (v6): targets are relative *remaining H+tail* costs of alternative exact two-edge matchings that share a provisional first edge, with independent simulator future tapes and a frozen C12 continuation. No future event, target cost or oracle label enters policy inference. The label itself is a continuation-specific estimate, not a calibrated machine value or optimal Q function. AirME/HetGPO already uses recursive assignments, STOP and shared entity encoders; neither those pieces nor value pretraining alone are novelty claims. The possible contribution is a tested, economically supervised conditional demand/capacity representation whose *within-tick update* improves closed-loop cost over matched frozen-context controls. Failure modes include noisy labels, off-policy C12 state coverage, choosing the first edge or STOP incorrectly, and inability to translate local rank into whole-episode benefit.

Keep the two pre-v13 fixed `n5k3_residual_a/b` configs from v12 (`family_config("n5_k3_sparse", 74740000/74740001, "full")`, H48+tail16), threshold PM, automatic CM, unchanged simulator law and physical matching/WAIT set. Internal decoder choices are provisional; only the completed matching advances physics/cost/failure tape. Historical N4/K2 V14 cannot score this 136-action N5/K3 panel and remains external. F1 risk and F2 six-tick rollout remain same-panel references.

## Simulator label construction and isolation

Use all three fixed DEV-selected **C12** checkpoints per case from the audited corrected-source v12 run. For each label raw seed `77701000..77701095`, case and C12 root, follow one deterministic C12 physical episode. Select the **first** tick in 8..39 where at least one legal first edge has at least two legal, higher-index, nonconflicting second edges. Choose one qualifying first edge by `role_seed(raw, "v15-first:<case>:<C12root>") % number_of_qualifying_first_edges` over sorted candidates; record every nonqualifying episode. Candidate physical actions are all exact two-edge matchings sharing that first edge. For each candidate, branch the unchanged simulator from this present state onto four common independent future event tapes, seeded `role_seed(raw, "v15-value:<case>:<C12root>:<tick>:<slot>")`, slot 0..3; continue with the **same C12 checkpoint** to H and risk tail. Record remaining H+tail cost and use its four-tape mean. State records contain present-state policy inputs, legal mask, selected first edge, candidate second indices/actions and branch-cost vector. Do not carry event tapes, future costs or teacher future state into actor input. Stop after one label state per raw episode; no label selection by economic spread.

The fixed training target within each record is `softmax(-mean_branch_cost / 20)` across its second edges. Each pretrained actor minimizes listwise cross-entropy over those edges only. This target does not supervise root choice, STOP, or third assignment; PPO must learn those from real episode reward. No reward shaping or altered physical action. Label generation is an additional simulator budget, reported separately and shared exactly by all pretrained arms. Require at least 50 labelled states per case and 150 total, otherwise record insufficient coverage and stop before learning. This is a coverage gate only; do not choose labels or alter temperature based on their costs.

## Arms, probability and training budget

C15 is the v14 canonical C actor with the same candidate-residual update, pretrained on the conditional labels and then PPO. D15 has **identical modules, raw information, canonical path and label/PPO budgets**, but freezes root candidate/STOP contexts inside the tick while updating feasibility; D15 is separately pretrained and trained. CF15 loads each selected C15 state_dict into D15 at inference. B15 uses direct entity features with the same label/PPO budget and canonical sequence. S15 structured and G15 flat full-information complete-action policies receive the **same label records**: their auxiliary listwise scores are those of the corresponding candidate two-edge *complete matching* actions. They then receive the same PPO budget. P15 is a canonical contextual actor trained by PPO from scratch with no auxiliary labels; it isolates the extra simulator supervision. Q15 is flat always-request PPO from scratch, an admission reference rather than an architecture attribution arm. F1/F2 are same-panel controls. The seven scratch arms are C/D/B/S/G/P/Q; C/D/B/S/G have identical per-case label records and 64 fixed pretraining epochs (batch 64, Adam 3e-4, cross-entropy, no label-based checkpoint selection), with separate seeds for parameter initialization. Reset to a fresh Adam optimizer before PPO so pretraining momentum is not carried into the RL updates. P/Q have no pretraining; this compute imbalance is explicit. No pretraining signal is used by the PPO reward or at inference.

C/D/B/P PPO uses unique increasing-edge token paths: exact conditional log probabilities sum to the physical matching log probability because each matching has one path. Sampled conditional entropy sums are path statistics, not full matching entropy. S/G/Q use one masked categorical over full matchings. Every arm has all physically legal matching/WAIT actions under its admission rule. Deterministic evaluation is tokenwise greedy for sequential arms, not necessarily the most probable matching. Each physical transition contributes reward/cost once. Verify exact old/new PPO ratios, masks, STOP/forced end, normalization, no future leakage, save/load, matching/kernel equivalence and CF weight identity.

Pretraining uses fixed 64 epochs and no validation checkpoint. Then train all seven arms from their specified initializations for **8,192 H decisions per case/arm/PPO root** with the v14 PPO settings (256-H rollouts, batch64, four epochs, Adam 3e-4, gamma .99, GAE .95, clip .2, entropy .01, gradient clip .5, one CPU Torch thread). Evaluate deterministic checkpoints at 2,048/4,096/8,192 on 16 DEV seeds; choose minimum mean H+tail, earliest tie. Two-hour timeout per trial including pretraining/DEV. No outcome-driven stopping. Report label branches, pretrain updates, training H decisions, actor/critic parameters, scorer passes, forecast transitions, selection/training wall time, availability, failures, queues, backlog, starts, WAIT, cardinality and skill allocation.
