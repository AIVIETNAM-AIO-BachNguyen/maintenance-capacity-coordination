## Motivation and scope

The audited L2 DEV panel is hypothesis-generating. At the same 96,000 controlled decisions and greedy deployment, S−F H+tail cost was −70.88 in base and −321.71 in generalist; S−A was −11.81 and −8.95. At 384,000 and 1,536,000, greedy S−F shrank to near zero or reversed. These observations suggest a possible **sample-efficiency** benefit of the structured actor over the flat actor, while the specific cross-message contribution remains uncertain. The post-DEV matched analysis and its descriptive, unadjusted intervals are in `artifacts/jcst_learnability/matched-dev-analysis-20261027T092654.270699Z/report.json`. These numbers determine the new question and are not confirmation evidence.

The primary question is whether a fixed 96,000-decision S actor beats each F and A at the **same** training budget, deployment mode, physical panel and PPO recipe in both base and generalist. S−F estimates the structured-actor package relative to a full-information flat actor; it is not an isolated message effect. S−A isolates cross-entity message inputs within the matched structured actor and is the mechanism contrast. Parameter counts and wall-clock/inference compute will be reported, so a difference is not silently attributed to exactly equal compute.

## Unchanged contract and arms

Use JCST/1.1 base/generalist, H=48 and fixed risk tail B=16, full current/public information, all complete legal matchings and WAIT, PM allowed on healthy components at all ages, target-only restoration, unchanged failure/cost/skill/duration laws and undiscounted H+tail physical cost. No future noise, fee, reward shaping, forced service or action restriction. S, F and A are the existing `src/ht_pdm_fjsp/jcst_learner_v1.py` actors and the same shared-architecture full-information critic: S has cross-entity component/technician messages; A zeroes the cross-entity message inputs but retains its own features, durations, modules and parameter count; F is a flat full-information complete-action scorer with injective edge-indicator plus count action code. All receive the same complete legal-action mask. Do not modify these actors or the simulator for L3.

All arms use the L2 PPO recipe: stochastic TRAIN collection, Adam learning rate 0.0003, four epochs, minibatch 128, clipping 0.2, entropy coefficient 0.01, global gradient norm cap 0.5, 25 episodes per update, reward divisor 100, gamma 1 and GAE lambda 0.95. Ten new, independent training roots per arm/case; no root selection. Every root trains from scratch through nested checkpoints 24,000, 96,000 and 384,000 controlled decisions (500, 2,000 and 8,000 full episodes). Checkpoints of one trajectory are not independent replicates. Physical TRAIN seeds are disjoint across arms and roots, and Torch seeds are disjoint from physical seeds. No tuning or intervention after collection begins.

## Fixed evaluation and statistics

The **primary endpoint** is 96,000-decision **greedy** deployment, with the existing normalized-logit argmax and lowest-catalogue-index exact-tie rule. Evaluate all ten roots per arm/case on the same 128 fresh physical seeds per case. Four primary contrasts are S−F and S−A in each case, using equal root and physical weights; negative cost difference favors S. Root-index numbers have no pairing meaning between arms. Secondary, predeclared analyses evaluate greedy 24,000 and 384,000 checkpoints, plus stochastic 384,000 with four independent action-sampling seeds nested within each physical seed. Evaluate threshold5, risk/duration matching, corrective-only and WAIT once on the same physical panel. Report H and tail cost separately, cost components, PM/CM, failures, downtime, backlog, forced/discretionary WAIT, training/inference wall time, parameter counts and all ten root means. No checkpoint/mode selection is performed on this panel.

For each primary contrast, independently resample ten training roots within each arm and jointly resample the 128 paired physical indices across arms; use 4,000 fixed-seed two-way crossed-bootstrap draws. Report two-sided percentile intervals with Bonferroni family adjustment over all four primary contrasts (`alpha=.05/4`, 98.75% per-contrast coverage). No pooled-case success substitution. A per-case contrast qualifies as a practical S advantage only if its observed mean is at most −10 simulator cost units **and** its adjusted upper bound is below zero. Claim S−F structured-actor sample-efficiency benefit across the core cases only if both S−F contrasts qualify; claim cross-message mechanism benefit only if both S−A contrasts qualify. Report one-case or statistically negative outcomes plainly. Ten units is the inherited L1/L2 practical scale (roughly 1.2–1.3% of threshold5 cost), not a monetary calibration. The 384,000 checkpoint, stochastic mode, controls and compute are secondary and cannot replace a failed primary gate.

## Seed roles and integrity

L3 physical seed base is `2^52`, Torch seed base `2^51`; these are outside the L2/C1/L1 banks. Define TRAIN physical seed as `2^52 + 10^13 + case_index*10^12 + arm_index*10^11 + root*10^8 + episode_index`, where case index is base 0/generalist 1, arm index S 0/F 1/A 2, root 0–9 and episode index 0–7,999. Define evaluation physical seed as `2^52 + 2*10^13 + case_index*10^12 + physical_index`, with physical index 0–127, identical across arms, roots, checkpoints, modes and controls. Torch model/init/training seed is `2^51 + 10^13 + case_index*10^12 + arm_index*10^11 + root*10^8`. Stochastic evaluation action seeds are 64-bit BLAKE2b digests of the UTF-8 string `JCST-L3:case:arm:root:checkpoint:physical_index:repetition` with repetition 0–3; action sampling never sets physical failure noise. Bootstrap RNG seed is `20261027` and independent of simulator and action RNG. Any accidental seed collision or role overlap invalidates affected L3 collection.

Before any outcome interpretation, an independent auditor must verify source/protocol/config/checkpoint hashes, complete expected row counts and unique seed keys; legal actions and policy-action probabilities; independent transition and H/tail cost replay for all evaluation episodes and fixed TRAIN traces; TRAIN/evaluation bank disjointness; and the raw mean, bootstrap and gate arithmetic. A self-check is not an independent audit. If integrity fails, preserve the affected run and stop that part; fix source only with explicit versioned correction and new run, never overwrite or relabel results.
