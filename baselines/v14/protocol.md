## Hypothesis and controlled mechanism

At a fixed current 56-coordinate observation and legal matching, V14 uses identical edge, machine, worker, demand and WAIT heads in both scratch-RL arms (3,219 parameters, every parameter gradient-active on a representative legal-multi-action state). Both price technician occupation against learned nonnegative demand. `competition` uses
`P_joint(a)=sum_(m,j in a) duration(m,j) sum_(n not served by a) d(n,j,s)`.
`count_calibrated` uses
`P_count(a)=sum_(m,j in a) duration(m,j) [(4−|served(a)|)/3] sum_(n != m) d(n,j,s)`.
The control preserves service cardinality and matches the candidate on one-service actions and on all legal actions under uniform machine demand. It removes only the identity of the *other* machine served by a multi-service matching. WAIT logits are identical at matched weights. No action restriction, future tape or planner call enters either deployed actor.

H1: the identity-specific term lowers four-case mean H48+tail16 economic cost by at least 10 versus count-calibrated, with gains in at least four of five training seeds including the three lowest-cost control seeds and 20,000-draw hierarchical 95% CI upper <0. H2 promotion additionally requires candidate ≤1.02× bounded rollout, ≥10× faster selection, and no risk-matching dominance in both cost and runtime. A DEV pass cannot establish goal A/B, novelty, independent replication or fresh confirmation.
