# Project instructions

Read RESEARCH_BRIEF.md and CURRENT_STATE.md first. They define this project's objective; old reports and imported source docstrings are historical context, not active goals. Maintain one current experiment and a concise state file. Do not import the old campaign journal wholesale.

The contribution is a maintenance-demand/technician-capacity coordination framework. Standard PPO/CTDE is acceptable. Do not require a new RL algorithm or structural requester necessity before testing learning benefits. Do not claim a two-policy framework exists until its roles and behavior are implemented and evaluated.

Preserve simulator semantics unless a separately justified operational contract and versioned protocol authorize a change. Do not manufacture requester benefits through fees, hidden information or forced shutdowns. Keep centralized full-information and always-request controls.

For research experiments, follow docs/experiment_workflow.md: prelock protocol, tests and local macOS smoke, then commit/push when authorized; provide lab commands rather than SSH or launching full runs. A direct explicit user override can authorize a lab agent to launch qualified runs. Do not infer that authorization from historical reports.

Use tqdm for training/multi-seed evaluation. Each run uses a fresh timestamped artifact directory; preserve failures and partials. Do not overwrite user work, alter raw results, tune on TEST or treat engineering fixtures as scientific evidence.

The migration smoke is engineering validation, not a research campaign. No full run or historical checkpoints should execute merely on setup. Do not commit/push unrelated files or create branches unless authorized.
